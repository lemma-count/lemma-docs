## Use the three work queues

- **All** — every Lead in the workspace.
- **Unassigned** — Leads with no current live Mission membership.
- **In Missions** — Leads assigned to one or more current live Missions.

The List sidebar is separate from these queues.

## Open Leads

[Open Leads](https://app.heylemma.com/leads)

Choose **Add leads** to import people or **Create list** to build a static List.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="A Lead is not automatically eligible">
Import or List membership does not override Do not contact, missing LinkedIn identity, active-outreach conflicts, Sender requirements, or a playbook’s relationship-state rules.
</HelpCallout>
