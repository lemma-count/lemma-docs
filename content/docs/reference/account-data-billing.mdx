Open [**Settings → Account & billing**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/account) for the current plan, usage, renewal state, and billing portal.

## Managed Lead limits

Plans count Leads managed by Lemma in Lemma-led Missions. Manual Sequences do not count toward the Managed Lead cap.

- **7-day trial** — up to 10 Managed Leads.
- **Starter** — $29/month and up to 50 Managed Leads.
- **Growth** — $99/month and up to 200 Managed Leads.
- **Scale** — $299/month and up to 1,000 Managed Leads.

The in-product Account & billing page is authoritative before purchase or a plan change.

When the cap is reached, Lemma blocks adding more Managed Leads to Lemma-led Missions. Existing Manual Sequences remain outside this cap.

## Manage the subscription

Choose **Manage billing** to open the Stripe billing portal when a customer account exists. The page also shows the current plan, Managed Lead usage, renewal or cancellation-at-period-end date, and payment problems.

Choose a plan card to subscribe or switch. Do not rely on an old screenshot or invoice for the current cap.

## Data access, export, retention, or deletion

The current outbound UI does not expose a verified general self-service export or account-deletion workflow. Do not treat any of these as data deletion:

- marking a Lead **Do not contact**;
- removing a Lead from a List;
- archiving a List;
- stopping a Sequence;
- completing or archiving a Mission.

Those controls preserve operational history.

Use the **Support** button in Lemma’s sidebar for access, export, retention, correction, or deletion questions. Include the affected Lead or Mission, but never send passwords, session cookies, provider tokens, or payment-card details.
