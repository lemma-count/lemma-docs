title: Execution evidence
description: Use the product layer and proof grade that describe what actually happened.

Do not turn a Mission phase, approval, queue entry, or internal completion into a claim about LinkedIn.

## Layer 1: Mission lifecycle

Public labels such as **Needs input**, **Draft**, **Active** or **Live**, **Paused**, **Finishing**, **Completed**, and **Failed** describe the Mission as a whole. A Mission can be Active or Live while some Sequences wait, fail, or finish.

## Layer 2: Outbox Sequence

The public Sequence summary uses:

- views: **Open**, **Needs you**, **Finished**;
- Open facets: **Ready**, **Scheduled**, **Waiting**, **Paused**, **Problems**;
- Finished outcomes: **Completed**, **Replied**, **Opted out**, **Stopped**.

These states summarize the Sequence. They are not direct evidence that each LinkedIn touch was sent.

## Layer 3: Individual touch

Inside **Planned touches**, each touch has its own state, schedule or condition, Sender, message, and history.

- A scheduled time is intent.
- A sending or unresolved attempt is not safe to repeat blindly.
- A failed task does not prove that LinkedIn accepted nothing.
- A stopped or canceled future touch remains in history.

## Layer 4: Provider confirmation

Use one of these for a past-tense delivery claim:

- **Provider confirmed** in the Sequence touch history;
- a **Provider confirmed** entry in the Audit log with a provider receipt.

The UI can explicitly say **Marked complete without provider confirmation**. Treat that as unconfirmed, not sent.

## Approval evidence

Approval permits a specific scope to progress. It is not an external action.

- Lemma-led Mission approval is a choice and request in the Mission conversation.
- **Validate sequence** in Outbox approves and freezes the current bodies of one eligible Lemma-authored Sequence; **Batch validate** applies the same boundary to eligible selections or a Mission.
- Manual Launch commits the exact wizard Review and queues approved work.
- A later material change may produce a new revision or require a new decision.

## Safe language

Use:

- “prepared” for content or a plan;
- “approved” for the reviewed permission;
- “scheduled” for a future time;
- “waiting” for a condition;
- “problem” for a blocked or failed Sequence;
- “provider-confirmed” when the receipt exists.

<HelpCallout type="insight" title="When evidence conflicts">
Preserve the most specific external evidence. Do not retry or rewrite history while provider confirmation is unresolved. Escalate with the Sequence and Audit entries.
</HelpCallout>
