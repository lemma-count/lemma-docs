---
title: Use the Audit log
description: Read a read-only timeline of committed controls and provider-confirmed LinkedIn actions.
---

Open [**Settings → Audit log**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/activity).

The Audit log combines two proof grades:

- **Control committed** — Lemma committed a Sender, Do not contact, Mission, or approval-policy change.
- **Provider confirmed** — a LinkedIn receipt confirms an invitation, message, or withdrawal.

## Events covered

The current log can include:

- Sender connected, reconnected, replaced, paused, or resumed;
- Sender daily target or active-window changes;
- Do not contact added or removed;
- Mission launched, paused, resumed, completed, or archived;
- Mission approval or execution-mode changes;
- LinkedIn work held or resumed;
- LinkedIn invitation sent, message sent, or invitation withdrawn.

## Read an entry

Each entry shows:

- a human-readable action;
- proof grade;
- **Operator** or **System** actor;
- UTC timestamp;
- a short summary;
- links or short IDs for the Mission, Lead, Sender, task, or provider receipt when available.

<HelpCallout type="insight" title="Use the proof grade">
A committed control proves Lemma accepted the control change. It does not prove LinkedIn performed an action. Use a **Provider confirmed** entry or the Sequence history for that claim.
</HelpCallout>

## Filter the log

Filter by **Action**, exact **Mission ID**, exact **Lead ID**, **From**, and **To** dates. Choose **Apply filters**. Use **Load 50 more** for the next page.

If a UUID filter is invalid, return to the unfiltered Audit log and copy the ID from the relevant Mission or Lead link.

## Unavailable or stale data

When Lemma cannot verify the complete audit union, it does not present a partial result as empty:

- **Unavailable** shows no partial history and offers **Retry**.
- **Stale** preserves the last verified page, labels it stale, and disables further paging until refresh succeeds.
