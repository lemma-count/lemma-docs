Use [**Settings → About offer**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/about-offer) to keep the facts behind the outreach in one reviewable dossier.

## What you can record

The offer dossier includes:

- company name and website URL;
- a one-line description;
- a longer company or offer description;
- unique selling points;
- proof points;
- pricing posture;
- an ideal-customer-profile hint.

## Save and validate the dossier

Choose **Save draft** while the information is incomplete or still under review. To choose **Validate**, add at least a company name and one-liner, then confirm that the visible facts are accurate.

Validation confirms the stored dossier; it does not approve a Mission or its messages.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Mission context still needs an explicit review">
Saving or validating **About offer** does not currently promise automatic reuse in Mission briefs, targeting, drafts, or personalization. Put the relevant offer facts in the Mission brief and verify every claim before execution.
</HelpCallout>

## Workspace scope

The dossier belongs to the active workspace. If you switch workspaces, check **About offer** again before preparing outreach.
