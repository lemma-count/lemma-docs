---
title: Sender, Lead, and approval boundaries
description: Understand the conditions that remain in force after preparation or approval.
---

Approval describes permission for a specific scope. It does not override the Sender, Lead, timing, hold, or provider conditions.

## Sender boundaries

- A healthy verified Connect or Replace normally becomes **Ready** automatically.
- Reconnect preserves the existing execution status.
- An exceptional **Dry run** Sender needs **Activate sending** when that control appears.
- A **Paused**, **Restricted**, or **Unavailable** Sender is not executable.
- Automatic effective-capacity warm-up starts with the first provider-authorized outreach activity: paid, Sales Navigator, and Recruiter Senders ramp from 10 to 25 invitations and 20 to 80 messages per day over 14 days; new, free, and unknown Senders ramp from 5 to 20 invitations and 10 to 40 messages per day over 21 days.
- The configured daily target is the desired ceiling. The effective limit is the current ramp- and provider-safe ceiling; the active schedule, confirmed activity, and reservations can reduce available capacity again.
- A provider retry boundary or account restriction can stop otherwise eligible work.
- Reconnecting does not silently resume an intentional pause.
- Replacing is blocked while non-terminal work references the current Sender, and existing tasks are never reassigned.

## Lead boundaries

- Do not contact prevents future executable outreach.
- A valid LinkedIn identity is required for LinkedIn work.
- Active outreach conflicts can block a Manual launch.
- A List or Mission assignment does not prove eligibility.
- Imported and inferred fields must not become claims beyond their available evidence.
- An invitation note is available only for paid, Sales Navigator, or Recruiter Senders and is limited to 200 characters. Free or unknown Senders must omit it.

## Approval boundaries

### Lemma-led

**Review approval** and **Auto approval** are Lemma-led Mission controls. Auto approval applies only to future LinkedIn Sequences; existing decisions do not change.

Eligible Lemma-authored Sequences also have a pre-send review boundary in **Outbox → Open**. Editing, adding, or deleting changes the active revision. Rescheduling one eligible touch re-anchors the eligible pre-send chain, while priority applies Sequence-wide to eligible pre-send tasks. **Validate sequence** or **Batch validate** approves and freezes the current message bodies. Sent, provider-confirmed, or otherwise locked touches cannot be rewritten through this editor.

### Manual

The wizard’s **Review** step checks the exact List revision, Sender, playbook, rendered copy, eligibility, and blocked reasons. **Launch Mission** commits that reviewed scope.

Neither path overrides Sender readiness, schedule, daily targets, Do not contact, eligibility, holds, or provider evidence.

## Stop boundaries

- **Stop Sequence** affects one future Manual cold Sequence.
- **Pause Manual Mission** affects Manual sending for one Mission.
- **Hold all manual sending** affects all Manual work, not Lemma-led work.
- **Hold LinkedIn** affects provider-side LinkedIn work for one Lemma-led Mission while Lemma can keep preparing.
- Sender **Pause** affects eligible work through that LinkedIn account.
- **Complete Mission** applies to one Lemma-led Mission: it cancels eligible unclaimed work but lets already-sending work settle.

## Outcome boundaries

Lemma does not guarantee replies, meetings, qualified Leads, revenue, deliverability, compliance, or platform safety.
