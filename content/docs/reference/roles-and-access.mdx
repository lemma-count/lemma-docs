title: Operator access
description: Understand the current full-access Operator model in Lemma.
---

**Operator** is Lemma’s public name for the person who sets up and controls outbound work. The underlying technical role may still appear as `sponsor` in older data or system diagnostics.

## Current access model

An Operator can access Leads, Missions, Outbox, Settings, and the controls available in Lemma.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="There is no read-only Operator access">
Treat Operator access as consequential access to Lemma and its outbound controls. Do not share access with someone who should only review results.
</HelpCallout>

## Before granting access

Confirm that the person is allowed to:

- view and organize Leads;
- read Mission briefs, research, and Drafts;
- review or change Sequences;
- act on replies and problems;
- change Sender and account settings.

Use the **Support** button in Lemma’s sidebar if the visible access does not match what you expect.
