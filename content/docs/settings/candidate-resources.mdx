---
title: Add candidate resources
description: Keep reusable booking links and company pages ready for candidate conversations.
---

Candidate resources are links your workspace can reuse in a recruiting Mission. Saving a resource does not send it to anyone or choose a Mission outcome.

## Before you start

Have the exact destination URL and a clear name, such as “Company careers page”. Open the destination yourself and check that candidates can access it.

## Add a link

1. Open [Settings → Candidate resources](https://app.speiros.com/settings/actions).
2. Choose **Add link**.
3. Select **Link type**: **Booking**, **Signup**, or **Custom**. Use **Custom** for approved company pages and other resources.
4. Fill in **Name** and **Destination URL**.
5. Optionally enable the default-link preference for that type. This is a workspace preference; each Mission still determines its use.
6. Choose **Add link** and confirm the resource appears as **Active**. Use **Open destination** to verify it.

Under **Suggested from your workspace**, **Use** opens an editable suggestion, such as your Sender booking link. Review and save it to create a reusable resource. Later profile or context changes never silently change that resource.

## Edit, disable, or remove

Use **Edit** and **Save link** to update the name or destination. An existing link's type stays fixed. **Make default** sets the preference for its type.

**Disable** stops selection for new Mission configuration and keeps the resource in Mission history. **Enable** makes a disabled resource active again. **Delete** is permanent and is available only when no Mission references the link. Disabling a link does not erase already shared messages or historical references.

## Check the result and recover

The link's state, default preference, and number of Missions using it are shown on its card. A tracked click does not establish signup, booking, or interview completion.

For **Links are unavailable**, choose **Try again**; do not assume the library is empty. Correct form errors before saving, or [contact Support](/help/support) if a link cannot be changed.
