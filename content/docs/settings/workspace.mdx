---
title: Switch workspaces and invite teammates
description: Work in the intended organization and manage invitations and access requests.
---

Check the workspace name before changing recruiting context, accounts, or team access. A workspace switch changes which organization's data and settings you are viewing.

## Before you start

You need operator access to manage invitations and organization settings. Invited teammates join as operators, so review who should have access to the workspace's recruiting work.

## Switch workspace

Open the workspace selector and choose the intended workspace. Confirm its name after the page refreshes. If you create a new workspace through the selector, provide a name of at least two characters and check that the new workspace is active.

## Invite a teammate

1. Open [Settings → Organization](https://app.speiros.com/settings/workspace) in the intended workspace.
2. In the team section, choose **Invite Members**.
3. Enter **Email address** in **Invite a teammate** and choose **Send invitation**.
4. Check the success message and **Pending Invitations**, including the address and expiry.
5. Ask the teammate to open the invitation, review the workspace and signed-in account, and choose **Accept invitation**.

The invitation page may allow acceptance with a different signed-in email, and displays a warning when addresses differ. Review that identity deliberately before accepting; do not assume the invitation forces a switch to the invited address.

## Manage pending access

Use **Revoke** in **Pending Invitations** to withdraw an unaccepted invitation. If a teammate sees **Invitation expired**, send a new invitation. **Already accepted** offers **Go to workspace**.

For a pending access request, review the person and choose **Approve** or **Reject**. These requests are distinct from invitations you sent.

## Check the result and recover

After acceptance or approval, check the active-member list and have the teammate verify workspace access. An email-send error can occur after an invitation was created; inspect pending invitations and revoke the failed invitation before sending a replacement.

If Settings redirects or the workspace is missing, check the signed-in account and selector. [Contact Support](/help/support) with the workspace name and visible error if access still fails.
