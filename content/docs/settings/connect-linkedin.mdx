Connecting LinkedIn lets Speiros read the account and prepare recruiting work. **Connecting alone sends no messages or invitations and publishes nothing on your behalf.** Outreach follows the actions and campaign rules you approve.

## What happens during connection?

Speiros opens a connection page on **connect.speiros.com**. Sign in with your LinkedIn credentials and complete any verification code or app approval requested by LinkedIn. Keep that page open until verification finishes, then return to Speiros to continue setup.

The connection page is operated by [Unipile](https://www.unipile.com/), our account connection provider. Your password is entered on that page and does not pass through Speiros’s servers. You can visit Unipile’s website to learn about the service and the integrations it provides.

This connects a session that can support reading and sending through your account. It does not, by itself, approve outreach or publish anything. This is why connection status and sending status appear separately in Speiros.

## Why am I asked to select a contract?

If LinkedIn exposes several Recruiter or Sales Navigator access contexts, the connection page may ask you to choose one. Unipile calls these **contracts**. Their names come from LinkedIn; seeing two entries does not by itself mean you purchased two subscriptions.

Choose the entry you normally use in LinkedIn. Only one contract is selected for the connected product at a time. That selection determines the Recruiter or Sales Navigator context used for subsequent requests and events. The list shows available access, not a guarantee that every license or session will remain valid.

Recruiter and Sales Navigator cannot both be activated on the same Unipile account. See [Unipile’s contract guide](https://developer.unipile.com/v2.0/docs/linkedin-manage-contracts) for the provider’s exact behavior. If you are unsure which entry to choose, [contact Support](/help/support) before reconnecting repeatedly.

### Change the selected contract

Open [Settings → Sender](https://app.speiros.com/settings/channels), select the connected LinkedIn Sender, and refresh its account status if requested. In **LinkedIn contract**, choose the access you normally use, then select **Change contract** and confirm. This changes the context for subsequent requests and events; it does not activate all contracts together.

Use **Check change status** to verify the result. A requested change is shown as in progress until LinkedIn confirms it. Refreshing the page preserves that pending change, so you do not need to submit it again. If the available list has changed, use **Refresh contracts** before making another selection.

## Before you start

Use an account you control and are authorized to use. Check the active workspace. If a Sender already exists and needs repair, use [Recover a Sender](/settings/recover-sender).

## Connect and verify

1. Open [Settings → Sender](https://app.speiros.com/settings/channels) and select **LinkedIn**.
2. Under **Connect your LinkedIn Sender**, choose **Connect** and complete the provider's verification flow.
3. Return to Speiros and check the displayed name and account identity.
4. Read both **Connection** and **Execution**, including the explanation beneath each badge.
5. Review the [sending schedule](/settings/schedule-capacity) before preparing outreach.

In onboarding, the starting button is **Connect LinkedIn**. If verification opens in another tab, return and use **Check status**. If the link expires, check whether the connection completed before choosing **Get a new connection link**.

## Check the result

The intended account should appear as **Connected**. Read the execution state separately: **Ready** permits eligible work within its schedule and limits. A connected account may still be paused, restricted, or waiting for validation. Connecting it does not approve a Mission's outreach.

If an older or exceptional **Dry run** state offers **Activate sending**, review the account and schedule before using it. Use **Resume sending** when you are ready to end an intentional pause.

## If verification does not settle

For **Sender readiness is unavailable**, choose **Retry**. A last verified state may remain visible but read-only until verification succeeds. If the connection flow was not completed, read the notice before restarting; do not assume the previous account was replaced.

If Speiros asks to move an account used elsewhere, review the [transfer consequences](/settings/recover-sender#transfer-an-account) first. For an incorrect identity or repeated failure, [contact Support](/help/support).

Next, [check LinkedIn product access](/settings/linkedin-access).
