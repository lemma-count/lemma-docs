A **Sender** is an account representing your hiring team. Its connection, sending permissions, execution state, and schedule each affect whether work can proceed. Available channels depend on connected accounts and the permissions available for the action; the setup guides below cover LinkedIn.

## Manage your workspace

- [Switch workspaces and invite teammates](/settings/workspace).
- [Manage your account and role capacity](/settings/account).

Open **Settings** from the account menu in [Speiros](https://app.speiros.com/settings/channels). Check the active workspace before changing its accounts or resources. If the page is inaccessible, [contact Support](/help/support) with the page and workspace you intended to open.
