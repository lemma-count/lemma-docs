---
title: Connect an interview calendar
description: Connect Google or Outlook and set the availability Speiros may use.
---

The meeting calendar supports interview coordination. Its availability settings are separate from the Sender's outreach schedule.

## Before you start

Use a Google or Outlook account whose calendar you are authorized to connect. Check the active workspace. Only one meeting-calendar connection is supported at a time.

## Connect and configure

1. Open [Settings → Calendars and coordination](https://app.speiros.com/settings/calendar).
2. Under **Target calendar**, choose **Connect Google Calendar** or **Connect Outlook Calendar** and complete authorization.
3. Return and check the provider, account, and **Connected** state.
4. In **Booking settings**, set **Timezone**, working hours and days, default meeting duration, buffers, and minimum notice.
5. Set **Target calendar id (optional)** only if you need a specific calendar. Leaving it empty uses the primary calendar.
6. Choose **Save booking settings** and check the confirmation.

## Interview-team access

A booking calendar and booking settings do not establish your interviewers’ availability. Each interviewer must authorize calendar access. To send interview invitations, the organizer calendar must also allow event changes. Use **Manage interviewers and organizer access**, or open [Settings → Interview team](/settings/interview-team), to configure these separately.

## Check the result

The intended calendar account is connected and your booking settings are saved. A connected calendar or shared booking link does not itself prove an interview was confirmed; check the actual scheduling result.

## Reconnect or disconnect

If the connection shows **Reconnect required**, choose **Reconnect**, authorize the same account, and recheck its state. **Disconnect** removes the connection after confirmation. To use a different provider, disconnect the current one before connecting another.

If authorization or saving fails, read the error and retry the appropriate operation. For repeated failures, [contact Support](/help/support) with the provider and visible message.
