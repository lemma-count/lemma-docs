---
title: Follow a role and its recruiting work
description: Use role states and mission history to keep each hiring need organized.
---

Use **Roles** to see which hiring needs are open and to find the recruiting missions attached to each one. A role's state is separate from the state of any mission.

## Before you start

You need an existing role in your current workspace. To add one, follow [define and open a role](/recruiting/create-role).

## Review the role

1. Open **Roles**. Current roles are grouped by recruiting state; **Archives** shows closed roles and their saved history.
2. Choose **View role** to inspect the role’s candidates and recruiting conversations. **Role conversation** opens the conversation about its brief.
3. Select the role title to open **Role details**, including its job description and process assignment. Source-managed descriptions must be edited in the source.
4. Follow recruiting conversations in the sidebar and use [Work](/work) for reviews, sending and candidate activity.
5. For an active role, select **Find candidates** to begin a sourcing conversation attached to that hiring need. **Contact candidates** prepares outreach from the role’s selected candidates or an existing list; it still requires subsequent cohort and message review.

## Close or reopen recruiting

When the role no longer needs recruiting, return to **Roles**, select **Close role**, and review the confirmation. Closing frees an open-role place while preserving the role and its sourcing history.

If Speiros reports active recruiting work, inspect the role's missions and [finish the relevant work](/work/lifecycle) before closing. Do not use role closure as an emergency stop for an action already in progress.

To recruit for the role again, open **Archives** and select the role’s reopening control, subject to available capacity and the source job's availability. Verify the state is Open before starting a new conversation.

## Keep changes in the right place

For a manually managed draft, open **Role details** and review **Title** and **Description** before **Save job description**. A role marked **Synced from your hiring system** is owned by its source: edit it there and synchronize. For jobs on your connected LinkedIn account, select **Import LinkedIn roles** in Roles to import current jobs and refresh their source status. Follow progress in **Updates**, then verify the role and its LinkedIn status. The synchronization also refreshes the connected account; it is more than a local page reload.

Check company or process changes separately in [Knowledge](/recruiting/review-context).

If the role or missions fail to load, refresh the page. If the source job is unavailable, synchronize its source status before trying to reopen it. A source job that remains closed or unavailable cannot be opened for recruiting just by retrying.

If **Import LinkedIn roles** reports that synchronization could not start, check the connected account in Settings. If it started but the page could not refresh, reload Roles and inspect **Updates** before starting another synchronization.

Continue with [search for candidates](/candidates/search) or [supervise a mission](/work).
