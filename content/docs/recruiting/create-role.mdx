---
title: Define and open a role
description: Create a role draft, review its brief, and explicitly open recruiting.
---

A role describes the hiring need. Creating its draft gives you something to review; Explicitly opening the role makes it available for a recruiting conversation. Neither action approves outreach or publishes a job posting.

## Before you start

Use the correct workspace and prepare the known role facts: title, responsibilities, requirements, location, and employment terms. Mark unknowns explicitly. Your plan must have an available open-role place to open recruiting. To import existing LinkedIn jobs, you also need the intended connected LinkedIn account.

## Import an existing LinkedIn role

If the job already exists on your connected LinkedIn account:

1. Open **Roles** and select **Import LinkedIn roles**. This starts synchronization and refreshes the connected account.
2. Follow the synchronization progress in **Updates**. A started synchronization is not confirmation that every job was imported.
3. Check the Roles list for the imported job, read its details and source status, then select the role’s opening control when it is available and you are ready.

Importing reads existing jobs; it does not publish a new LinkedIn job posting. If the role is source-managed, edit the original job in its source and synchronize again.

## Create a draft with Speiros

1. Open **Roles** and select **Create a role**. This is also available in the **Choose a role** dialog.
2. Enter **Role title** and select **Create draft**. Speiros creates a free draft and opens its dedicated role conversation.
3. Supply the role brief or reference document there. Ask Speiros to keep confirmed facts separate from points to verify. For example: “Prepare a brief for a backend engineer in Paris. PostgreSQL experience is required; compensation is not confirmed yet.”
4. Open **View role** to inspect the hiring need. Its title opens **Role details**, including the job description and process assignment.
5. For a manually managed draft, review **Title** and **Description**, then choose **Save job description** when editing directly.
6. Return to Roles and select the opening control for that role: **Open role** or **Review role access**, depending on its state. Review capacity and any confirmation before recruiting activation.

## Check the result

The open role is available in **Find candidates → Choose a role**. Its details page also offers **Find candidates**, keeping the new conversation attached to that role.

Publishing a context section and opening a role are separate decisions. A role brief can be ready while its role is still Draft.

## If opening is blocked

If synchronization cannot start, inspect the connected account in **Settings → Communication accounts**. If synchronization started but the page could not refresh, reload Roles and check **Updates** before starting it again.

Check the displayed role capacity. If all places are in use, close a role whose work is finished or [review your account](/settings/account). A role managed by a hiring system must be edited there and synchronized; direct job-description edits are for manually managed drafts. A source job that is closed or unavailable cannot be treated as an open recruiting opportunity.

Continue with [search for candidates](/candidates/search) or [manage a role](/recruiting/manage-role).
