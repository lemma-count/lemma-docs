---
title: Define and open a role
description: Create a role draft, review its brief, and explicitly open recruiting.
---

A role describes the hiring need. Creating its draft gives you something to review; **Open recruiting** makes the role available for a recruiting conversation. Neither action approves outreach or publishes a job posting.

## Before you start

Use the correct workspace and prepare the known role facts: title, responsibilities, requirements, location, and employment terms. Mark unknowns explicitly. Your plan must have an available open-role place to open recruiting. To import existing LinkedIn jobs, you also need the intended connected LinkedIn account.

## Import an existing LinkedIn role

If the job already exists on your connected LinkedIn account:

1. Open **Roles** and select **Import LinkedIn roles**. This starts synchronization and refreshes the connected account.
2. Follow the synchronization progress in **Updates**. A started synchronization is not confirmation that every job was imported.
3. Check the Roles list for the imported job, read its details and source status, then select **Open recruiting** when it is available and you are ready.

Importing reads existing jobs; it does not publish a new LinkedIn job posting. If the role is source-managed, edit the original job in its source and synchronize again.

## Create a draft with Speiros

1. Open **Roles** and select **Create a role**. You can also choose **Create a role** from the **Start recruiting** dialogue.
2. In the Recruiting context conversation, describe the role or ask for an empty draft. You can supply a public job-posting URL as a reference.
3. Ask Speiros to keep confirmed facts separate from points to verify. For example: “Create a draft for a backend engineer in Paris. The role requires PostgreSQL experience; compensation is not confirmed yet.”
4. Open **Roles** and find the new role under **Draft**.
5. For a manually managed draft, select **Edit draft**, review **Role title** and **Role details**, then **Save draft**. An **Untitled role** needs a title before recruiting can open.
6. Select **Open recruiting**. Confirm that the role now appears as **Open**.

## Check the result

The open role is available in **Start recruiting → Choose a role**. Its details page also offers **Start recruiting**, keeping the new conversation attached to that role.

Publishing a context section and opening a role are separate decisions. A role brief can be ready while its role is still Draft.

## If opening is blocked

If synchronization cannot start, inspect the connected account in **Settings → Sender**. If synchronization started but the page could not refresh, reload Roles and check **Updates** before starting it again.

Check the displayed role capacity. If all places are in use, close a role whose work is finished or [review your account](/settings/account). A role managed by a hiring system must be edited there and synchronized; **Edit draft** is for manually managed drafts. A source job that is closed or unavailable cannot be treated as an open recruiting opportunity.

Continue with [search for candidates](/candidates/search) or [manage a role](/recruiting/manage-role).
