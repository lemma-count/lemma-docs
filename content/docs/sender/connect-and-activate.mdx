---
title: Connect a LinkedIn Sender
description: Connect the correct LinkedIn identity and verify its current execution state.
---

Connect the one LinkedIn profile you want Lemma to use for outbound in the active workspace.

<Outcome title="Outcome">
The intended account appears under **Settings → Sender**. A healthy verified connection normally shows **Connected** and **Ready**.
</Outcome>

## Before you start

Use an account you control and are allowed to use. If you work across several LinkedIn profiles or Lemma workspaces, confirm both before continuing.

## Connect the account

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open LinkedIn settings

Open [**Settings → Sender**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels). If no Sender exists, the page shows **Connect your LinkedIn Sender**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose Connect

Choose **Connect** and finish the provider-hosted authentication flow. Return to Lemma after the provider window completes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for verification

The connection is not authoritative until provider verification settles. A healthy connection normally becomes **Ready** automatically.

If Lemma cannot verify the latest state, it preserves the last verified view as read-only and offers **Retry**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify the identity

Check the displayed Sender name, LinkedIn identity, connection badge, and provider status. If the identity is wrong, do not queue more work. Follow [Replace the account](/sender/pause-reconnect-replace#replace-the-account).

</Step>
<Step>

### Review the active schedule

Check **Invitations/day**, **Messages/day**, active days, start and end times, and timezone. The daily targets are desired ceilings; the current effective limits can be lower while capacity warms up or another safety limit binds. Choose **Save settings** after a change.

</Step>
</Steps>

## If the Sender remains in Dry run

**Dry run** is an exceptional or older state. If **Activate sending** is available:

1. verify the account identity and schedule;
2. choose **Activate sending**;
3. confirm the execution badge changes to **Ready**.

Do not look for a generic **Activate** button on every new connection.

## Ready and full capacity are different

A Ready Sender can execute eligible work, but its effective invitation and message limits warm up automatically. The ramp begins with the first provider-authorized outreach activity, not when the account connects or becomes Ready.

Read [Sender readiness, schedule, and capacity](/sender/readiness-and-schedule) before increasing the first Mission’s audience.

## Other outcomes

### Restricted

Read the execution reason shown directly under the state. Resolve that account or provider condition; there is no separate “state detail” screen to open.

### Unavailable execution

Read the reason under the execution badge. Depending on the verified state, **Reconnect**, **Replace**, or schedule controls can remain available. **Retry** is not the general recovery action for this badge.

### Sender readiness is unavailable

Choose **Retry** when the page uses this exact heading. Lemma cannot verify an authoritative page state, so execution controls are absent. A stale projection similarly keeps the last verified state read-only until **Retry** succeeds.

### Reconnect is rejected

A disconnected Sender can show **Reconnect** even when its stored provider identity proof is incomplete. If Reconnect is rejected or Settings cannot verify that it is the same LinkedIn identity, do not retry repeatedly. Use **Support**.

### The connection flow failed

The previous Sender and execution state are not replaced. Start **Connect**, **Reconnect**, or **Replace** again only after confirming which operation you intended. Do not use **Connect** to repair an existing disconnected Sender.
