---
title: Pause, reconnect, or replace a Sender
description: Stop sending, restore the same account, or move future work to a different LinkedIn identity.
---

Choose the smallest action that matches what changed.

## Pause or resume

Use **Pause** when you want the Sender to stop becoming eligible while keeping its identity and current plan visible.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Inspect current work

Open [Outbox](https://app.heylemma.com/outbox). Note any touch already in progress and any Sequence waiting on this Sender.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pause the Sender

Open [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels) and choose **Pause**. Confirm the execution badge becomes **Paused**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Resume deliberately

After the reason for the pause is resolved, choose **Resume sending** and confirm the state becomes **Ready**. Reconnecting the account is not a substitute for resuming an intentional pause.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Reconnect the same account

Choose **Reconnect** when the provider session for the same LinkedIn identity expired or became unusable.

After the provider flow:

1. verify that the LinkedIn identity did not change;
2. check the execution state and inline reason — Reconnect preserves the existing execution status;
3. inspect upcoming Sequences before retrying any action.

A paused Sender stays paused after reconnecting. Reconnect is not a substitute for **Resume sending**.

## Replace the account

Choose **Replace** only when the workspace should use a different LinkedIn identity.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Replacement can be blocked">
Lemma rejects replacement while planned or in-flight work still references the current Sender. Existing tasks are never reassigned to the new account.
</HelpCallout>

If replacement is blocked:

1. open the affected Mission and Outbox Sequences;
2. hold, stop, complete, or let the referenced work settle as appropriate;
3. retry **Replace** only after no non-terminal work references the Sender.

A successful healthy replacement normally makes the new Sender Ready after verification. Recheck the identity, schedule, daily targets, Missions, and upcoming Sequences. Approval for one Sender does not automatically describe the operational intent for a different identity.

## If an external outcome is unclear

Do not reconnect, replace, or retry merely because an internal state is slow to update. Open **History and provider evidence** for the affected Sequence. A duplicate LinkedIn invitation or message is harder to undo than a deliberate wait.

## Next action

If the Sender is healthy but work still cannot progress, follow [Resolve a Sequence problem](/outbox/resolve-problems).
