A **Ready** Sender is usable, not unlimited. Lemma still applies its schedule, daily targets, reservations, provider conditions, and the state of each Sequence.

## Read the Sender card

Open [**Settings → Sender**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels).

<TwoColumn>
  <Panel title="Connection">
    Shows Connected or Disconnected, the provider explanation, provider status, and the last checked time.
  </Panel>
  <Panel title="Execution">
    Shows Dry run, Ready, Paused, Restricted, or Unavailable, plus the current reason and any next eligible time.
  </Panel>
</TwoColumn>

If the projection is stale, the page shows the last verified state and disables controls until **Retry** succeeds.

## Read sending capacity

Invitations and messages are reported separately:

- **Daily target** — the configured desired ceiling;
- **Effective limit** — the current ramp- and provider-safe ceiling;
- **Confirmed** — provider-confirmed actions in the current daily window;
- **Reserved** — capacity held by active claims;
- **Remaining** — capacity that can still be considered after confirmed activity, reservations, and conservative provider-authorized attempt counting.

The **Binding safety limits** card appears when another limit is tighter. Lemma may perform fewer actions than the configured target.

## Understand the automatic capacity warm-up

The warm-up applies to effective capacity and starts with the first provider-authorized outreach activity, not when you connect the Sender.

| Sender class | Invitation ceiling | Message ceiling | Ramp period |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Paid, Sales Navigator, or Recruiter | 10 → 25 per day | 20 → 80 per day | 14 days |
| New, free, or unknown | 5 → 20 per day | 10 → 40 per day | 21 days |

The configured daily target remains your desired ceiling. Increasing it does not bypass the ramp: Lemma uses the lower applicable limit. Actual available capacity can be lower again because of the active schedule, provider conditions, confirmed activity, or reservations.

## Configure the active schedule

<Steps>
<Step>

### Set daily targets

Enter whole numbers of zero or more in **Invitations/day** and **Messages/day**. A target is the desired ceiling, not a promise to use all available capacity or a way to bypass the current effective limit.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose active days

Select at least one day. Lemma should wait when a touch falls outside an active day.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set the window and timezone

Set **Start**, **End**, and a valid timezone such as `Europe/Paris`. The Sender’s schedule is evaluated in this configured timezone.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save and verify

Choose **Save settings**, then return to [Outbox](https://app.heylemma.com/outbox) to inspect the next known touch.

</Step>
</Steps>

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Outbox uses two different clock displays">
Outbox formats timestamps and date groups in **Europe/Paris**, even when the Sender schedule uses another timezone. Its editable **Send date** field uses the browser’s local timezone. Home activity and the Audit log use UTC. Confirm which clock produced a value before diagnosing or rescheduling an early or late action. See [How Lemma displays time](/reference/timezones).
</HelpCallout>

## Why work can wait

Common reasons include a future scheduled time, a closed active window, exhausted capacity, an intentional pause, a provider retry boundary, a pending LinkedIn event, or another active claim.

Lemma also spaces and varies otherwise eligible Sender actions. The schedule and daily target define boundaries, not an exact delivery cadence.

**Waiting** does not mean an external attempt failed. Open the Sequence to read its timing condition and history.
