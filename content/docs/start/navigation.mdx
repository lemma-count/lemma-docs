The sidebar follows your recruiting work. Its navigation may show icons or labels depending on its width; use the named controls or their tooltips to choose an area.

## Main navigation

| Area | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Roles** | Create or import a hiring need, review its brief, and open its candidates and recruiting conversations. |
| **Process** | Define reusable interview steps and participants, then assign a process to a role. |
| **Knowledge** | Maintain company context, provide resources, and review proposed changes before publication. |
| **Needs you** | Open the Work page for decisions, replies, sending progress, and candidate activity. |
| **Candidates** | Inspect saved people, import candidates, organize lists, and use personal views. |

The panel beneath the navigation reflects the selected area. Role entries expose their brief and conversations; other areas expose their own objects or conversations. A role conversation prepares the hiring need. A sourcing conversation searches for candidates. Knowledge conversations maintain shared context.

To start from a hiring need, open **Roles**, choose **View role**, then **Find candidates**. Where **Find candidates** opens **Choose a role**, choose an open role and continue. **Search without a role** supports exploration; preparing a recruiting mission still requires an open role. **Create a role** creates a draft and opens its dedicated role conversation.

## The three Work views

- **Needs you** contains reviews, replies, and other decisions waiting for you.
- **Sending plan** contains sequences and their sending state.
- **Candidate activity** shows recorded interactions and available evidence.

The Work checklist shows your next setup tasks. **Getting started** in the account menu returns to it. Its optional product tour explains the main concepts in a compact card that continues across pages. Pause and resume it from the header; [onboarding](/start/onboarding#discover-the-product-and-resume-the-tour) explains the controls. Completing the tour does not complete recruiting tasks.

## Account and workspace controls

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar for **Settings**, **My account**, **Billing**, **Getting started**, **Documentation**, and **Support**.

Settings separates **Recruiter profile**, **Calendars and coordination**, **Interview team**, **Communication accounts**, **Candidate resources**, **Integrations**, **Do not contact**, and **History**, alongside your account and billing pages. Interviewers are distinct from people invited to access the Speiros workspace.

Use the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar to change space. Before approving work, check that the workspace contains the company context and accounts you intend to use.

## If you cannot find something

Use Roles for a hiring need, Candidates for saved people, and Sending plan for a sequence. If a page cannot load, use its retry control and check the workspace. A failed load does not prove work disappeared.
