The main navigation follows the recruiting work. Start a search from **Start recruiting**, then return to **Work** to continue conversations and handle decisions.

## Main navigation

| Area | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Work** | Continue a recruiting conversation, handle decisions and replies, inspect sending, or view candidate activity. |
| **Roles** | Follow the roles you are hiring for and their recruiting missions. |
| **Candidates** | Inspect saved people, import candidates, organize lists, and use personal views. |
| **Recruiting context** | Maintain company and role context, examine sources, and review suggested changes. |

**Start recruiting** opens **Choose a role**. Choose an open role to tie the search to that hiring need. **Create a role** takes you to Recruiting context. **Explore without a role** lets you investigate before choosing a role; preparing a mission requires an open role.

## The three Work views

- **Needs you** contains reviews, replies, and other decisions waiting for you.
- **Sending plan** contains sequences and their sending state.
- **Candidate activity** shows recorded interactions and available evidence.

Some detailed panels still use **Outbox**, **Mission**, or **Lead**. Sending plan is the work previously described as Outbox, and Candidates are the people considered for recruitment.

## Account and workspace controls

Open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar for **Settings**, **Support**, and **Account & billing**. Settings includes **Sender**, **Candidate resources**, **Integrations**, **Do not contact**, and **Audit log**.

Use the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar to change space. Before approving work, check that the workspace contains the company context and accounts you intend to use.

## If you cannot find something

Use Start recruiting for a new search, Candidates for saved people, and Sending plan for a sequence. If a page cannot load, use its retry control and check the workspace. A failed load does not prove work disappeared.
