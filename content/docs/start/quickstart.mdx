---
title: Run your first Mission
description: Connect LinkedIn, add a small Lead set, create one Mission, and verify what happens next.
---

Start with 5–10 Leads. A small first Mission makes the audience, copy, timing, and controls easy to inspect.

<Outcome title="Outcome">
You will have one focused Mission, a known LinkedIn Sender, a small Lead set, and a clear review path. If you allow execution, you will know where to find provider-confirmed evidence.
</Outcome>

## If Lemma opens onboarding

New workspace Operators are sent to the five-step onboarding flow. Follow [Complete first-time onboarding](/start/onboarding). It creates a Lemma-led Mission and opens its Cockpit.

## If you already use the workspace

<Steps>
<Step>

### Check the Sender

Open [**Settings → Sender**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels). Confirm the LinkedIn identity, **Ready** state, active schedule, and daily targets.

A healthy new connection normally becomes **Ready** after verification. If you see **Dry run**, choose **Activate sending**. If you see **Paused**, choose **Resume sending** only after the reason for the pause is resolved.

Compare each configured daily target with its **Effective limit**. Capacity warms up automatically from the first provider-authorized outreach activity, and reservations or the active schedule can reduce what remains. [Read the capacity model](/sender/readiness-and-schedule#understand-the-automatic-capacity-warm-up).

</Step>
<Step>

### Add a small Lead set

Open [**Leads**](https://app.heylemma.com/leads) and choose **Add leads**. Use **LinkedIn search** for a people-search, Sales Navigator search, or profile URL. Use **Upload CSV** for a spreadsheet.

LinkedIn search starts as soon as you choose **Start import**; it does not have a separate preview screen. Spreadsheet import does include mapping, row review, conflict review, and confirmation.

[Choose an import path](/leads)

</Step>
<Step>

### Create the Mission

Choose [**New Mission**](https://app.heylemma.com/missions/new).

- Choose the Lemma-led path when you want Lemma to clarify the goal and prepare the plan with you.
- Choose **Build manually** when you already have an active List, a ready Sender, and the exact LinkedIn copy and timing.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give the Mission one clear goal

State what you sell, who should care, the result you want, and the claims or audiences Lemma must avoid.

<ExampleBlock title="A useful starting brief">
We help independent design studios reduce unpaid project overruns. Reach studio founders in France who recently grew their team. Keep the first message concise and do not promise guaranteed savings.
</ExampleBlock>

</Step>
<Step>

### Review in the correct place

For a **Lemma-led Mission**, continue the conversation in **Cockpit**. Keep **Review approval** selected while you inspect the first plan, then approve the exact Mission-level request in the conversation.

After Lemma creates Sequences, open them under **Outbox → Open**. Eligible Lemma-authored Sequences let you edit, add, or remove unsent touches; re-anchor the eligible pre-send chain while preserving its spacing; set priority for the Sequence’s eligible pre-send tasks; and choose **Validate sequence**. Validation approves and freezes the active Sequence revision for that prospect. You can also use **Batch validate** for a selection or, with a Mission filter, the whole Mission.

For a **Manual Mission**, the wizard’s **Review** step is the binding check. **Launch Mission** commits that reviewed audience and queues approved work. There is no guaranteed second approval gate after Launch.

</Step>
<Step>

### Monitor Outbox

Open [**Outbox**](https://app.heylemma.com/outbox) after Sequences exist. Use:

- **Open** for current work;
- **Open → Problems** for blocked or failed work;
- **Needs you** for rows with an action Lemma can currently offer;
- **Finished** for completed, replied, opted-out, or stopped outcomes.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify the first external action

Open the Sequence and expand **History and provider evidence**. A schedule, queue entry, approval, or completed internal task is not proof that LinkedIn accepted an action. Treat it as sent only when provider confirmation is shown.

If you are evaluating Lemma without contacting anyone, pause or hold the relevant work before a Sender and Sequence become eligible.

</Step>
</Steps>

## If the path stops

- **No Sender is available:** connect or recover it under [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels).
- **No Manual List is available:** create a static List with 1–5,000 Leads. See [Create and manage Lists](/leads/create-manage-lists).
- **A LinkedIn import is waiting:** check its card under **Recent imports** for Pending, Running, Paused (rate-limited), Completed, Failed, or Canceled.
- **A Mission still needs clarification:** answer the focused request in Cockpit.
- **Work has a problem:** open **Outbox → Open → Problems** and follow the action that is actually available.
- **You are unsure whether an action happened:** do not retry until the Sequence history or provider evidence makes the outcome clear.
