---
title: Prepare your first Mission
description: Confirm a Sender, add 5–10 Leads, and create one focused Mission from your current Lemma setup.
---

Use this path when Lemma already opens its normal navigation. If Lemma opens the five setup screens instead, follow [Complete first-time onboarding](/start/onboarding).

<Outcome title="Outcome">
You will reach one Mission with a known Sender, a small Lead set, a clear goal, and an explicit review boundary.
</Outcome>

## Prepare the Mission

<Steps>
<Step>

### Confirm the Sender

Open [**Settings → Sender**](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels). Confirm the LinkedIn identity, **Ready** state, active schedule, and effective limits.

A healthy connection normally becomes Ready after verification. If you see **Dry run**, choose **Activate sending**. If you see **Paused**, resume only after you understand why it stopped.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add 5–10 Leads

Open [**Leads**](https://app.heylemma.com/leads) and choose **Add leads**.

- Use **LinkedIn search** for a people-search, Sales Navigator search, or individual profile URL.
- Use **Upload CSV** for a spreadsheet with mapping and row review.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create one focused Mission

Choose [**New Mission**](https://app.heylemma.com/missions/new).

- Choose the Lemma-led path when you want Lemma to clarify the goal and prepare the plan with you.
- Choose **Build manually** only when you already have the exact audience, copy, timing, and active Sender.

</Step>
<Step>

### State the goal and boundary

Include what you sell, who should care, the result you want, and claims or audiences Lemma must avoid.

<ExampleBlock title="A useful first brief">
We help independent design studios reduce unpaid project overruns. Reach studio founders in France who recently grew their team. Keep the first message concise and do not promise guaranteed savings.
</ExampleBlock>

For a Lemma-led Mission, keep **Review approval** selected while you inspect the first plan in the Mission conversation. For a Manual Mission, the wizard’s **Review** step is the binding check; **Launch Mission** commits the reviewed audience and queues approved work.

</Step>
</Steps>

## If preparation stops

- **No Sender is available:** connect or recover it under [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels).
- **No Manual List is available:** [create a static List](/leads/create-manage-lists) with a small first audience.
- **A LinkedIn import is waiting:** inspect its card under **Recent imports**.
- **The Mission needs clarification:** answer the focused request in its conversation.

Do not expand the audience yet. Continue to the first Sequence review.
