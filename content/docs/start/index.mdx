## What you need before the quickstart

- A LinkedIn account you are allowed to connect.
- A real offer and a clear audience.
- Five to ten people you may legitimately contact.
- Time to inspect the first audience, research, and messages before you let work progress.

<HelpCallout type="note" title="Healthy connections normally become Ready">
When provider verification succeeds, a newly connected Sender normally becomes **Ready** automatically. **Activate sending** appears only when a Sender remains in **Dry run**. Always verify the identity, schedule, and limits under **Settings → Sender**.
</HelpCallout>

Ready is not the same as full daily capacity. Invitation and message limits ramp automatically from the first provider-authorized outreach activity. [Understand the effective limit](/sender/readiness-and-schedule#understand-the-automatic-capacity-warm-up).

## What happens after setup

Lemma can clarify the brief, organize research, and prepare LinkedIn Sequences. For a Lemma-led Mission, choose **Review approval** or **Auto approval** in the Mission Cockpit. Outbox then shows each Lead’s Sequence, timing, problems, replies, and delivery evidence.
