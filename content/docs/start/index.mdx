Use this path if you are opening Lemma for the first time or want to rebuild a clear mental model before your next Mission.

<Outcome title="Your first complete loop">
Learn the five core objects, prepare a small real Mission, review its first Sequence, and verify the first external outcome from provider evidence.
</Outcome>

## Choose your path

Both paths converge at the same review step. Pick one:

<TwoColumn>
  <Panel title="First time in Lemma">

  </Panel>
  <Panel title="Already using Lemma">
    Follow [Prepare your first Mission](/start/quickstart). Confirm an existing Sender, add a small Lead set, and create one focused Mission.
  </Panel>

## What you need

- A LinkedIn account you are allowed to connect.
- A real offer and a clear audience.
- Five to ten people you may legitimately contact.
- Time to inspect the first audience, research, messages, and timing.

<HelpCallout type="note" title="Keep the first loop review-first">
Use a small Lead set and keep **Review approval** selected for a Lemma-led Mission. A healthy Sender may become Ready automatically, but prepared or approved work is still not proof that LinkedIn accepted an action.
</HelpCallout>

After the first loop, use [Home](/start/home) for orientation or browse the dedicated Sender, Leads, Missions, and Outbox collections.
