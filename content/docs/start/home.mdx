[Home](https://app.heylemma.com/home) is a product summary. It is not an approval queue.

## Outbound outcomes

The first section covers the rolling last 30 days across Mission history:

- distinct Leads contacted;
- distinct Leads who replied;
- Leads classified as interested;
- reply rate, calculated as replied Leads divided by contacted Leads.

Choose the linked outcome or reply-rate tile to open Mission performance.

## Outbox now

The current queue shows:

- **Provider-proven sent** — provider-confirmed LinkedIn actions today;
- **Scheduled** — Sequences currently scheduled;
- **Problems** — Sequences that need resolution.

Choose a tile to open the matching Outbox view. Problems open under **Open → Problems**, not the **Needs you** view.

## Recent external interactions

This table shows provider-confirmed messages and invitations, connection acceptances, and inbound replies. Choose a Mission name to open it.

Home activity times are shown in **UTC**. Outbox currently shows times in **Europe/Paris**. See [How Lemma displays time](/reference/timezones).

## When a card cannot load

Each Home section fails independently. Choose **Retry** on the affected card. A failed summary card does not change or stop the underlying Mission.

## Next action

Use Home for orientation, then open [Outbox](/outbox) for the current queue or [New Mission](https://app.heylemma.com/missions/new) to start work.
