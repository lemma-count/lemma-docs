Lemma is a professional outbound product for independent professionals, founders, and lean teams.

You define what you sell, who should care, the outcome you want, and the boundaries. Lemma helps organize Leads, research them, prepare relevant LinkedIn outreach, and move permitted work forward without hiding the plan or the next decision.

## The operating idea

  <Panel title="Lemma prepares">

  </Panel>

  </Panel>

## The basic workflow

<Steps>
<Step>

### Set the boundary

Connect a LinkedIn Sender, add Leads, and define the offer, audience, outcome, and constraints in a Mission.

</Step>
<Step>

### Prepare relevant work

Lemma clarifies the brief, organizes available research, and prepares a Sequence and messages tied to the Mission.

</Step>
<Step>

### Review what matters

For Lemma-led Missions, you choose **Review approval** or **Auto approval** from the Mission. For Manual Missions, you approve the exact audience and rendered copy in the wizard’s **Review** step before Launch.

</Step>
<Step>

### Monitor execution truth

Missions and Outbox keep planned work, provider-confirmed sends, replies, blockers, failures, and next actions visible.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What Lemma does not imply

Lemma does not guarantee replies, meetings, revenue, qualification, deliverability, or platform safety. A generated message, approval, or scheduled time is not a send. Execution remains subject to Sender readiness, timing, pacing, daily targets, active hours, Do not contact rules, and provider conditions.

<HelpCallout type="note" title="Current channel scope">
These guides cover LinkedIn Sender setup and execution. Leads enter through the documented import paths; Lemma does not source them automatically. Email execution and CRM actions are outside this Help Center.
</HelpCallout>

## When Lemma is a fit

Use Lemma when you own outbound directly and want researched preparation plus visible control. It is especially useful when the cost of generic outreach is high, but running a manual sales operation would consume too much attention.
