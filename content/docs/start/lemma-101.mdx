Lemma turns an offer and target audience into researched, reviewable LinkedIn outreach. You choose the account, audience, Mission boundary, approval mode, and what may progress.

<Outcome title="What you will understand">
You will know where work starts, where you make decisions, where you review messages, and what counts as proof that an external action happened.
</Outcome>

## The five objects

<TwoColumn>
  <Panel title="Sender">
    The LinkedIn account Lemma is allowed to use, with its connection state, schedule, targets, and effective limits.
  </Panel>
  <Panel title="Lead">
    A person you may contact. Leads can be added from LinkedIn or a spreadsheet and organized into Lists.
  </Panel>
  <Panel title="Mission">
    A bounded outbound goal: one audience, offer, Sender, constraints, and approval mode.
  </Panel>
  <Panel title="Sequence">
    The ordered LinkedIn touches prepared for one Lead, including copy, timing, current revision, and history.
  </Panel>
  <Panel title="Outbox">
    The operational queue. Review Sequences, resolve problems, handle replies, and inspect provider evidence.
  </Panel>
</TwoColumn>

## How work moves

<Steps>
<Step>

### Prepare

Connect a Sender, add a small Lead set, and create a focused Mission. Lemma can then clarify the brief, research the audience, and draft Sequences.

</Step>
<Step>

### Approve

In a Lemma-led Mission, its conversation contains the Mission-level approval boundary. In Outbox, **Validate sequence** approves and freezes the active Sequence revision for that Lead.

</Step>
<Step>

### Schedule

Approved work can become scheduled when the Mission, Sequence, Sender, timing, and safety conditions allow it. Scheduled still does not mean sent.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify

Treat an external action as sent only when the Sequence history shows provider-confirmed evidence. A draft, approval, task completion, or queue entry is not that proof.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Where you make decisions

| Decision | Place |
| --- | --- |
| Which LinkedIn identity, schedule, and limits may be used | **Settings → Sender** |
| Who belongs in the audience | **Leads** and Lists |
| What the outreach should achieve and avoid | Lemma-led Mission conversation |
| Whether a Lemma-led Mission uses Review or Auto approval | Lemma-led Mission |
| Whether the active message revision is acceptable | **Outbox** Sequence |
| What happened externally | Sequence **History and provider evidence** |

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Prepared is not sent">
Keep these boundaries separate: Lemma can prepare work; you can approve it; the system can schedule it; only provider evidence confirms that LinkedIn accepted the action.
</HelpCallout>
