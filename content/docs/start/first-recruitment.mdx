title: Your first recruitment
description: Go from company context and an open role to selected candidates, reviewed outreach, and a confirmed action.
---

Keep the first selection small enough to review every person's evidence and each planned message. This guide connects the tasks; follow the linked articles for the detailed controls.

## Before you start

You need access to the intended workspace and a LinkedIn account with the product access required for your search. Before sending, the Sender must be ready and eligible under its schedule and capacity. [Finish or resume onboarding](/start/onboarding) if setup is incomplete.

## 1. Establish the context and role

Open **Recruiting context** and describe the company and hiring need. Review suggested changes before accepting them. Explain the work, relevant backgrounds, and acceptable trade-offs.

[Create and open the role](/recruiting/create-role). A draft role must be opened before it can anchor mission preparation.

## 2. Start and refine the search

Choose **Start recruiting**, select the open role, and continue. Explain the backgrounds and evidence to investigate in the recruiting conversation.

Review profiles and refine the direction before retaining people. Follow [Search with Speiros](/candidates/search) and [Review a candidate profile](/candidates/review-profile). A preview is a sample, not an approved contact list.

## 3. Prepare a mission and confirm candidates

Bring the people you want to consider into an active static list. Ask Speiros to [prepare a mission](/work/prepare-mission) from that list and the open role.

Review the proposed cohort and [confirm the exact candidates](/candidates/confirm-selection). The draft mission exists before confirmation. Confirming people starts research and planning; it does not approve messages.

## 4. Review the work and controls

In the mission, clarify the approach and inspect [research and drafts](/work/review-drafts). Check the authorized accounts and channels.

For a first run, use **Review approval** to examine the sequences. Open **Sending plan**, inspect a candidate's sequence, and [approve it](/work/approve-sequence) when the intended actions and content are correct.

## 5. Verify what happened

Return to [Sending plan](/work/sending-plan). Approved work can still wait for a window, capacity, or another condition. Read its state and explanation.

Use provider-confirmed evidence to verify the first external action. Approval, a scheduled time, or Ready is not proof of delivery. For blocked sending, use [Recover a sequence](/help/sequence) rather than creating another copy.

## 6. Continue from Work

Use **Needs you** for reviews and replies, and **Candidate activity** for recorded interactions. Review a suggested answer before sending and keep it tied to the receiving account.

Your first result is a confirmed action and a clear next step. An interview or hire requires its own evidence; it does not follow automatically from a message.
