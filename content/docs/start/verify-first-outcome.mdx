Open the reviewed Sequence and expand **History and provider evidence**.

<Outcome title="Outcome">
You will identify the strongest available evidence for the first action and know whether to wait, resolve a problem, or treat it as provider-confirmed.
</Outcome>

## Read the four boundaries

| State | What it proves | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Prepared** | Research or a Draft exists for review | Approval, scheduling, or delivery |
| **Approved** | The relevant review boundary was accepted | That the action is queued or sent |
| **Scheduled** | The action has an intended execution time | That LinkedIn accepted it |
| **Provider-confirmed** | Provider evidence records the external action | A reply, interest, or business outcome |

## Decide from the evidence

<Steps>
<Step>

### If provider confirmation is present

Treat the action as sent. Use the Sequence history for timing and external evidence, then monitor replies in Outbox.

</Step>
<Step>

### If the action is scheduled

Wait for the scheduled time and Sender conditions. Do not infer delivery from the schedule alone.

</Step>
<Step>

### If a problem is shown

Open **Outbox → Open → Problems**. Follow the available recovery action and preserve any provider evidence already recorded.

</Step>
<Step>

### If the result is ambiguous

Do not retry. Repeated attempts can duplicate an external action. Check the Sequence history again or use Lemma support when the evidence cannot establish the outcome.

</Step>
</Steps>

<HelpCallout type="note" title="Evaluating without contacting anyone">
Pause or hold the relevant work before the Sender and Sequence become eligible. A connected Sender can be Ready even when you do not intend to allow execution.
</HelpCallout>
