title: Review your first Sequence
description: Inspect the Lead, message revision, timing, and approval boundary before work can progress.
---

Open the Mission’s first Sequence under [**Outbox → Open**](https://app.heylemma.com/outbox). Keep the first audience small enough that you can inspect every row.

<Outcome title="Outcome">
You will know exactly what is prepared for one Lead, whether the current revision is acceptable, and what validation changes.
</Outcome>

## Review one Lead end to end

<Steps>
<Step>

### Confirm the Lead and Mission

Check that the person belongs in the intended audience and that the Sequence belongs to the expected Mission. Stop and correct the audience before editing copy if either is wrong.

</Step>
<Step>

### Inspect every unsent touch

Read the message, invitation note, and follow-ups in order. Check:

- accuracy and relevance;
- claims and promises;
- tone and personalization;
- the spacing between eligible touches;
- anything the brief said to avoid.

</Step>
<Step>

### Edit the active revision

For an eligible Lemma-authored Sequence, you can edit, add, or remove unsent touches; re-anchor the eligible pre-send chain while preserving its spacing; and set priority for its eligible pre-send tasks.

</Step>
<Step>

### Validate only when it is ready

Choose **Validate sequence** to approve and freeze the active Sequence revision for that prospect. **Batch validate** applies the same decision to a selection, so use it only after the sample represents the rest of the audience.

</Step>
</Steps>

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Validation is not delivery proof">
Validation approves the active revision. It does not prove that LinkedIn accepted a message or invitation. Sender conditions, timing, Mission state, and other execution checks still apply.
</HelpCallout>

## If the Sequence cannot progress

Open **Outbox → Open → Problems** and follow the action that is actually available. Do not retry an ambiguous external action until Sequence history or provider evidence makes the outcome clear.
