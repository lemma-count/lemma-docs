title: Complete or resume onboarding
description: Complete initial setup, then use the Work checklist to prepare your first recruitment.
---

Initial setup has three stages: **Welcome**, **LinkedIn**, and **Your company**. You can postpone a stage and return later. Setup does not automatically create or approve a recruiting mission.

## Welcome

Answer how you heard about Speiros, or postpone the stage. This answer is separate from the company context used for recruiting.

## LinkedIn

Choose **Connect LinkedIn** and complete verification for the account Speiros should represent. Return and use **Check connection** if the connection is not yet reflected.

If the link expires, check whether the account connected before requesting another link. **Start again with a new link** begins a new verification attempt; it does not prove the previous connection failed.

You can complete this later under **Settings → Communication accounts**. Follow [Connect a LinkedIn Sender](/settings/connect-linkedin) for connection and readiness details. Connected does not mean an action was sent.

## Your company

Review the suggested company or use **Other company** when it is wrong. Supply the relevant website and choose **Build my context**.

Speiros prepares company context for review. Check the company and facts before accepting and publishing suggestions in **Knowledge**. A personal recruiter profile is different from the company whose role you are filling.

## Continue after initial setup

Open **Needs you** to reach Work. **Get started with Speiros** shows your next useful recruiting tasks: validate the recruiter profile, confirm professional contact details, publish reviewed company knowledge, connect your LinkedIn account, open a role, add candidates, and verify a first confirmed contact. It recognizes existing workspace facts; clicking a task or finishing a tour does not mark it complete.

The checklist separates **Your first recruitment** from **Prepare your interviews** and **Additional resources**. Calendar, interview-team and Slack setup are optional until you coordinate interviews; they do not prevent sourcing. Follow [Interview team](/settings/interview-team) when coordination becomes relevant.

Use **Show all 7 steps** to see the full recruiting checklist and **Refresh setup status** to recheck it. An unavailable status needs a retry; it does not prove a connection is missing.

## Discover the product and resume the tour

Choose **Discover Speiros** in the header or Work checklist. **Replay product tour** restarts a completed visit. The tour opens a small card alongside the relevant navigation and introduces **Roles**, **Candidates**, **Knowledge**, **Process**, then **Needs you**.

Each card explains the concept, shows an example, and suggests where to act. Choose **Next** to open the next area with the tour still visible, or **Back** to return to the previous step. You can explore the current page without completing a recruiting action. If you navigate elsewhere, the card offers a link back to the area it explains.

Choose **Pause**, close the card, or press **Escape** when you want to work freely. The header’s **Resume tour · 2/5**, for example, returns to your saved step and its page. Work also offers **Resume product tour** and **Restart tour**. **Finish tour** completes the visit; it does not mark setup tasks done or authorize outreach.

Progress can be resumed in the same browser for the signed-in user and workspace. Use **Getting started** in the header or account menu to return to the checklist whenever needed.

## Resume a postponed step

- Open **Settings → Communication accounts** to connect or recover an account.
- Use **Knowledge** to add company information and review changes.
- Return to **Work** for outstanding setup prompts.

If a stage cannot verify its state, use its retry or connection-check control. If it remains blocked, open **Support** from the account menu and describe the stage and error.
