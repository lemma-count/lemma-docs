title: Complete first-time onboarding
description: Follow the five setup screens that create your first Lemma-led Mission.
---

Lemma sends a new workspace Operator through onboarding before opening the normal product shell.

<Outcome title="Outcome">
You will connect LinkedIn, add a small Lead set, create one Lemma-led Mission, and land in its Cockpit.
</Outcome>

## Complete the five screens

<Steps>
<Step>

### Welcome

Answer **How did you find Lemma?** or skip the question. This answer does not change the outbound setup.

</Step>
<Step>

### LinkedIn

Choose **Connect LinkedIn** and complete the provider window for the profile you want Lemma to use.

- A healthy verified connection normally appears as **Connected** and ready.
- If the account is shown as **Paused**, choose **Resume LinkedIn** only when you intend to make it eligible.
- If **Reconnect needed** or another non-ready state appears, do not repeatedly choose **Connect**. Open **Settings → Sender**. The onboarding control cannot safely recover every existing Sender state; use **Support** if Settings cannot verify the existing identity or onboarding remains blocked.

You can change the schedule and daily targets later under [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels).

Becoming Ready does not make the full configured target immediately available. Effective invitation and message limits begin their automatic warm-up with the first provider-authorized outreach activity, not during connection.

</Step>
<Step>

### People

Add about 5–10 people for the first Mission. Paste one `linkedin.com/in/…` profile URL per line, then choose **Add people**.

This onboarding screen accepts pasted LinkedIn profile URLs only. It does not upload CSV files. Company pages, posts, and search-result URLs are not individual profiles.

</Step>
<Step>

### Mission

Describe one outreach goal in plain language. The screen shows the selected Sender and Lead count. Continue to create a Lemma-led Mission.

</Step>
<Step>

### Next

Choose **Chat with Lemma** to open the new Mission. Clarify the offer, audience, constraints, and plan in Cockpit.

</Step>
</Steps>

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Cockpit and Outbox approve different scopes">
Use Cockpit to choose **Review approval** or **Auto approval** and answer the current Mission-level **Approve** or **Deny** request. After Lemma creates Sequences, use Outbox to edit and validate eligible Lemma-authored Sequences before they send.
</HelpCallout>

## If you skipped a setup step

- Reopen [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels) for the Sender.
- Open [Leads](https://app.heylemma.com/leads) and choose **Add leads** for more people.
- Open [New Mission](https://app.heylemma.com/missions/new) if onboarding did not create the Mission.
- Use the **Support** button in Lemma’s sidebar if onboarding cannot advance.
