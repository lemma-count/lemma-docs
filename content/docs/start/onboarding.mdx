---
title: Complete or resume onboarding
description: Set up Welcome, LinkedIn, and Your company, then review the context Speiros prepares.
---

Initial setup has three stages: **Welcome**, **LinkedIn**, and **Your company**. You can postpone a stage and return later. Setup does not automatically create or approve a recruiting mission.

## Welcome

Answer how you heard about Speiros, or postpone the stage. This answer is separate from the company context used for recruiting.

## LinkedIn

Choose **Connect LinkedIn** and complete verification for the account Speiros should represent. Return and use **Check connection** if the connection is not yet reflected.

If the link expires, check whether the account connected before requesting another link. **Start again with a new link** begins a new verification attempt; it does not prove the previous connection failed.

You can complete this later under **Settings → Sender**. Follow [Connect a LinkedIn Sender](/settings/connect-linkedin) for connection and readiness details. Connected does not mean an action was sent.

## Your company

Review the suggested company or use **Other company** when it is wrong. Supply the relevant website and choose **Build my context**.

Speiros prepares company context for review. Check the company and facts before accepting and publishing suggestions in **Recruiting context**. A personal Sender profile is different from the company whose role you are filling.

## Resume a postponed step

- Open **Settings → Sender** to connect or recover an account.
- Use **Recruiting context** to add company information and review changes.
- Return to **Work** for outstanding setup prompts.

If a stage cannot verify its state, use its retry or connection-check control. If it remains blocked, open **Support** from the account menu and describe the stage and error.

## Next step

Follow [Your first recruitment](/start/first-recruitment) to create an open role, search for candidates, and prepare reviewable work.
