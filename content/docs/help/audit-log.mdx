**Settings → Audit log** records supported workspace control changes and provider-confirmed LinkedIn actions. Use it alongside the candidate’s sequence history, not as a complete transcript of every provider interaction.

## Before you begin

You need access to the correct workspace. To narrow a record, use the exact Mission or candidate identifier when you have it; the filter fields currently say **Mission ID** and **Lead ID**.

## Find an event

1. Open **Settings → Audit log**.
2. Choose an **Action**, or leave **All actions** selected.
3. Optionally enter **Mission ID** or **Lead ID**, and select **From** and **To** dates. The end date must be on or after the start date.
4. Click **Apply filters**. Use **Clear** to remove the filters.
5. Read the event’s action, actor, summary, timestamp, and linked references. Use **Load 50 more** when offered.

Supported action labels include Sender changes, sending-window and limits changes, Do Not Contact changes, Mission lifecycle and approval-mode changes, and confirmed LinkedIn invitations, messages, and withdrawals.

## Read the evidence grade

**Control committed** records a completed control change. **Provider confirmed** records the confirmed external action represented by that event. A committed approval-mode change is not proof of a message send.

Feed timestamps display UTC. Open the linked Mission or candidate when you need the exact sequence context, and compare the timezone when reading its diagnostics.

## If history is unavailable

Choose **Retry**. If the screen says it is showing the last verified audit page, treat it as stale; loading more is disabled until refresh succeeds. **No matching audit events** applies to a fresh filtered result, not a failed load. Broaden the filters before concluding the event is absent.
