---
title: Diagnose a blocked or failed sequence
description: Identify the execution condition before retrying or changing a plan.
---

Use the current sequence and its evidence to identify why the next touch has not happened.

## Before you begin

Know the intended candidate and Recruiting mission. Avoid creating replacement outreach while the outcome of an existing task is uncertain.

## Check the current sequence

1. Open **Work → Sending plan**, filter to the Mission, and inspect **Problems**, **Waiting**, or **Paused** as appropriate.
2. Open the candidate’s row. Read **What happens next**, **Planned touches**, and the displayed blocker.
3. Inspect **Execution diagnostics**, **Proof**, and **Durable history**. Check whether a send is provider-confirmed, still queued, or uncertain.
4. Verify the assigned Sender’s current connection and schedule in **Settings → Sender**, and the Mission’s **Contact channels** authorization.
5. Check the candidate’s Do not contact status and any existing conversation before planning further contact.

## Resolve the correct condition

- A reviewable draft needs [sequence approval](/work/approve-sequence).
- A relative follow-up can wait for a prior confirmed send; read its condition rather than inventing a date.
- A Mission or LinkedIn hold needs the matching [lifecycle control](/work/lifecycle).
- A manual global LinkedIn stop can offer **Resume sending**. That action does not clear automatic or scoped protections.
- An eligible failed task can offer **Retry task**. Retry that task rather than launching a duplicate sequence.

## If the result remains uncertain

Refresh current state and read the provider evidence again. **Partial evidence** or **Ambiguous truth** is not confirmation of either success or safe failure. [Contact Support](/help/support) with the sequence and visible diagnostic references before another send.

## Result and next step

Check the updated next action and history. A recovered task can still wait for execution gates. [Follow Sending plan](/work/sending-plan) until the outcome is recorded.
