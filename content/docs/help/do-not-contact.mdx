---
title: Manage Do not contact
description: Stop candidate outreach and understand exactly what Undo changes.
---

Do not contact is a candidate restriction across channels and Recruiting missions. It differs from closing a reply item, holding LinkedIn, or revoking one Mission’s channel permission.

## Before you begin

You need workspace operator access and the correct candidate record. Confirm the person’s identity before changing contact restrictions.

## Stop further outreach

1. Open the candidate’s detail from **Candidates**.
2. Check the current contact state and choose **Mark Do not contact** when contact must stop.
3. Verify the resulting **Do not contact** state. You can inspect the workspace registry in **Settings → Do not contact**.

A conversation in **Needs you** also offers **Ne plus contacter** (Do not contact). **Ne pas répondre** (Do not reply) closes the conversation item without making that same global contact decision.

## Undo a global restriction

Use Undo only when the candidate may be contacted again.

1. Open **Settings → Do not contact** and find the candidate.
2. Click **Undo** and read **Allow future outreach…?**.
3. Confirm **Undo Do Not Contact**.
4. Check the updated candidate state and any remaining channel-specific opt-outs.

The candidate detail can also offer **Undo Do Not Contact**. Undo removes the global restriction only. It preserves channel-specific opt-outs and outreach history. It does not re-arm canceled tasks, enrollments, or Missions, and it does not itself send a message.

## If the registry is stale

Use **Retry**. Undo is disabled while the registry is stale so that you do not act on an unverified state. A load failure is not evidence that no candidate is blocked.
