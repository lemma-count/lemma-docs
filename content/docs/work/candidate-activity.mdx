---
title: Read Candidate activity
description: Interpret relationship signals without confusing them with recruiting outcomes.
---

**Work → Candidate activity** shows informational relationship signals. It helps you inspect activity; decisions and replies belong in **Needs you**.

## Before you begin

You need workspace access. Available signals depend on the connected provider and what has actually been recorded; this page does not imply every channel is available for sending.

## Inspect activity

1. Open **Work → Candidate activity**.
2. Review the candidate scope. Choose **All activity** to include unmatched provider signals.
3. Read the event title, channel, and time. Follow a candidate or Mission link when the event has an exact recorded association.
4. Use **Mark activity as seen** on a new item when you have reviewed it.
5. Use **Load more activity** when offered to read earlier records.

## Interpret the signal

A **Message read receipt** or **Message reaction observed** reports a provider event. **Email open measured** and **Email link click measured** are tracking signals; they do not identify a physical reader or clicker. **Qualified link clicked** records an attributed click, not a booked interview. **New relationship observed** reports a relationship change. **Email delivery failed** reports a delivery failure, not a successful message.

An unmatched event can appear as **Not linked to a Lead**. Do not infer a candidate or Mission from a similar name. Marking activity as seen changes its review state; it does not approve outreach or respond to a candidate.

## If the list is empty or unavailable

An empty candidate view can exclude unmatched signals; check **All activity**. If the section says **Activity is unavailable**, retry loading it. A failed load is not evidence of no activity.
