**Work → Needs you** collects replies and decisions requiring operator review.

## Before you begin

You need access to the correct workspace. A review item may take you to its owning Mission or context; it is not necessarily a message ready to send.

## Handle an item

1. Open **Work → Needs you** and review **Needs you now**.
2. Read each review item’s title and detail, then choose **Review** to open the relevant surface.
3. Check its role, candidate, and Mission before making the requested decision. For a sequence, inspect the plan before approving it.
4. For a conversation, check the candidate, Mission, channel, and receiving account shown on the card. Read the recorded messages before editing or sending a response.
5. Return to Needs you and check the current queue after acting.

**You’re all caught up** means the queue currently contains no displayed decisions or replies. It is not a report that all recruiting work is complete.

## Choose the appropriate response

A conversation can offer **Envoyer** (Send), **Plus tard** (Later), **Ne pas répondre** (Do not reply), and **Ne plus contacter** (Do not contact). These have different effects. See [Reply to a candidate](/work/replies) before choosing one.

A sequence review link may still say **Open Outbox**. It opens the Sending plan rather than a separate inbox.

## If the queue cannot load

Use **Retry** on the unavailable section. Do not conclude that there is nothing to do from a failed load. If an action reports that the conversation changed, refresh and inspect the current messages before acting again.
