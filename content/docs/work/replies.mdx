---
title: Reply to a candidate
description: Review a conversation, send a response, or record a contact decision.
---

Review candidate replies in **Work → Needs you**. Responses use the actual receiving account, channel, and conversation. A reply stops the current sequence; do not assume follow-up outreach will continue.

## Before you begin

You need operator access and a current conversation. Check the candidate, Mission, channel, and account shown on the card. Some controls currently appear in French; their labels below match the screen.

## Review and send

1. Open the conversation in **Needs you**. Read the recorded messages; use **Afficher les … messages précédents** to reveal earlier messages when offered.
2. Review the suggested response. Edit it in **Écrire une réponse…**, or write your own if no response was prepared.
3. Check the exact message and receiving account, then click **Envoyer** (Send).
4. If you change your mind, use **Annuler** (Undo) during the 10-second cancellation window.
5. Check the resulting send status. Queued work is not a confirmed send.

Undo cancels the queued response during that window; it does not recall a message already sent. Recorded conversation history may not contain every earlier exchange from the provider.

## Other decisions

| Control | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Plus tard** — Later | You want to retain the conversation for later review. |
| **Ne pas répondre** — Do not reply | You want to close this item without sending a response. |
| **Ne plus contacter** — Do not contact | Further outreach to this candidate must stop across channels and Missions. |

Closing without a reply is distinct from Do not contact. A channel permission change is also distinct from a candidate contact decision.

## If sending fails or the conversation changes

Refresh before acting on a changed conversation. **Réessayer** (Retry) appears only for a recoverable failure; use it for the existing identified task. If the send outcome is uncertain, read execution evidence before attempting another response.
