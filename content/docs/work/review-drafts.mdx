Review the candidate’s evidence and the wording of the proposed sequence before deciding whether contact is appropriate.

## Before you begin

The Mission must have a confirmed candidate selection and research or drafts to inspect. Check that you are reviewing the intended role and candidate.

## Review the work

1. Open the Recruiting mission and read the conversation, including questions or decisions awaiting you.
2. Open **People** to inspect the selected candidates, then open a person’s detail to review their research and proposed next step. Use **Pulse** for the Mission journal.
3. If context or wording needs correction, explain the change in the Mission conversation. Identify the candidate and the factual correction precisely.
4. When a sequence is ready, follow its link to **Sending plan**. Check the candidate, Mission, account, **What happens next**, and **Planned touches**.
5. Where the current unsent sequence offers editing, change its text and choose **Save new revision**. Review the full revised plan before approving it.

Eligible sequences may also offer **Remove touch** or **Add touch**. Use only controls offered for the current sequence, and only a channel confirmed available and authorized for this Mission. A saved revision is a draft change, not proof of sending.

## If research or editing is unavailable

A missing draft does not mean an agent is currently working. Read the Mission’s current status and any requested decision. If a sequence has sent or locked work, editing controls can be absent. Do not create a replacement sequence merely to bypass that restriction; review the execution history first.
