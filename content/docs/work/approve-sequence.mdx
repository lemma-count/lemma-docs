---
title: Approve a sequence
description: Review the exact plan and authorize eligible candidate outreach.
---

Approval authorizes the current sequence for execution. It does not confirm an external send.

## Before you begin

You need an eligible sequence, operator access, and the intended Mission’s account and channel permissions. Review the [research and drafts](/work/review-drafts) first.

## Approve one sequence

1. Open **Work → Sending plan**.
2. Filter to the intended Mission and open the candidate’s sequence.
3. Check the candidate, account, **Planned touches**, and **What happens next**. Read any relevant execution history.
4. If changes are needed and editing is available, save a new revision and review it again.
5. Click **Approve Sequence** when it is offered. Check the updated sequence state and next action.

If you do not want the proposed outreach, use **Refuse** where offered. Stopping a sequence is different from recording a global Do not contact decision.

## Approve several sequences

Select only eligible rows you have reviewed, then use **Batch approve (n)**. Confirm that the number matches your intended selection. Review the resulting states; a stale or ineligible sequence should not be treated as approved just because you submitted a batch.

## If approval is unavailable

The current sequence may have changed, have no reviewable revision, or require a different action. Refresh and reopen its current detail. Resolve account or authorization errors instead of assuming approval bypasses them.

## Result and next step

Approved work remains subject to schedules, limits, relationship state, and provider readiness. **Ready** or an estimated **Now** is not a confirmed send. [Follow Sending plan and execution evidence](/work/sending-plan).
