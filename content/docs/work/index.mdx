Work brings together **Needs you**, **Sending plan**, and **Candidate activity**. A Recruiting mission keeps one hiring objective, its selected candidates, research, and contact plan together.

## Choose your next task

- [Prepare a Mission](/work/prepare-mission) after researching candidates for an open role.
- [Set Mission controls](/work/mission-controls) before allowing contact.
- [Review research and drafts](/work/review-drafts), then [approve a sequence](/work/approve-sequence).
- [Read Sending plan](/work/sending-plan) to distinguish planned work from confirmed sends.
- [Handle Needs you](/work/needs-you) and [reply to candidates](/work/replies).
- [Review Candidate activity](/work/candidate-activity) for informational signals.
- [Pause, resume, or finish a Mission](/work/lifecycle).

Candidate selection, draft preparation, approval, and provider-confirmed sending are separate steps. Connecting a Sender does not authorize that account for every Mission.

## Existing manual Missions

For a Mission created through an earlier manual workflow, use [Maintain an existing manual Mission](/work/existing-manual-missions). New recruiting starts with **Start recruiting**.
