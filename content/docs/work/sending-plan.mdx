Use **Work → Sending plan** to follow candidate contact plans. Some links still call this screen **Outbox**.

## Before you begin

You need workspace access and a Mission with draft or execution work. An empty plan is not evidence that outreach has occurred.

## Follow a sequence

1. Open **Sending plan** and choose the Mission filter, or search for a candidate or Mission.
2. Use **Open** for current sequences and **Closed** for completed or stopped work.
3. Filter current work by **Needs you**, **Ready**, **Scheduled**, **Waiting**, **In conversation**, **Paused**, or **Problems**.
4. Open a row and read **What happens next** and **Planned touches**.
5. Inspect **Execution diagnostics**, **Proof**, and **Durable history** to establish what actually happened.

## Read timing and evidence

**Now** or **Ready** means eligible work can enter execution; account gates still choose the actual sending instant. An estimated time is approximate. **Not before** is a lower bound. A later touch may wait for a prior confirmed send or another relationship condition before its timing becomes absolute.

The diagnostics distinguish **Current canonical truth**, **Projection update pending**, **Partial evidence**, and **Ambiguous truth**. Missing or uncertain evidence is not a successful send. Diagnostic times currently display **Europe/Paris**; check the timezone before comparing them with a Sender schedule.

## If progress looks wrong

Use the refresh control to read current state, then inspect **Problems** and history. Retry only an eligible identified task using **Retry task** when offered. If the outcome is uncertain, inspect its evidence before requesting another send. [Diagnose a sequence](/help/sequence) if the problem remains.
