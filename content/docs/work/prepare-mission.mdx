title: Prepare a recruiting Mission
description: Move from an open role and a candidate list to a confirmed Mission cohort.
---

Prepare a Mission when you are ready to research and plan contact with a specific group of candidates.

## Before you begin

You need an open role, a Sourcing conversation linked to that role, an active static list containing 1–50 candidates, and a connected LinkedIn Sender. You must have operator access to the workspace. **Explore without a role** supports exploration; it is insufficient for preparing a Mission.

## Prepare and confirm

1. Click **Start recruiting**, choose an open role in **Choose a role**, and click **Start recruiting**. If necessary, use **Create a role** first.
2. In the recruiting conversation, identify the exact active list and explain the hiring objective. Ask Speiros to prepare a Mission from that list.
3. Review **Proposed audience**. Search within the proposal, select individual people, or use **Select all visible**. Use **Clear selection** to start again.
4. Select at least one candidate and click **Confirm audience**.
5. Follow the Mission continuation shown in the conversation to review research and planning.

Preparation creates a draft Mission and proposed cohort before your confirmation. **Confirm audience** enrolls your selection for research and planning. It does not approve a sequence or send a message.

## If confirmation is blocked

If a selected candidate belongs to another active Mission, review the named Mission or choose **Review affected Leads**. Moving a candidate cancels unsent work there; already authorized provider work may still finish. Do not retry the same conflicting selection repeatedly. If the proposal changed, reload it and review the current selection.
