---
title: Pause, resume, or finish a Mission
description: Choose the correct control for agent work, sending, and Mission closure.
---

Choose a control according to what you want to stop. These controls affect different scopes.

## Before you begin

Open the intended Mission and check its current state. For a workspace-wide sending hold, use Sending plan and verify its scope before acting.

## Choose a control

| Control | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Stop** in the conversation | Requests cancellation of the observed agent turn. Background tasks already admitted can continue. |
| **Pause after this turn** | Lets current Mission work settle and prevents new Mission turns from starting. |
| **Hold LinkedIn** | Applies the Mission’s LinkedIn activity hold. |
| **Revoke** a contact channel | Blocks future sequence sends on that authorized channel while preserving history. |
| **Complete Mission** | Ends new Mission work and preserves the plan, evidence, history, and memberships. |

For a candidate who must receive no further outreach, use [Do not contact](/help/do-not-contact).

## Pause and resume

1. Open the Mission and click **Pause after this turn**.
2. Check **Paused after this turn**. Current work may still finish.
3. When ready, click **Resume** and check the updated Mission state.

To hold only LinkedIn activity, open **More mission actions** and select **Hold LinkedIn**. Use **Resume LinkedIn** to clear that Mission hold. This is separate from cancelling an agent turn.

## Finish the Mission

1. Open **More mission actions → Complete Mission**.
2. Read the confirmation and choose **Complete Mission**.
3. Wait for already authorized activity to settle. The finishing view is read-only.
4. Read the terminal report and preserved history.

Completing a Mission does not retract messages already sent or guarantee cancellation of provider work already authorized.

## If sending remains paused

In Sending plan, **Resume sending** clears a manual global LinkedIn stop. It does not clear automatic or scoped protections. Inspect the remaining explanation rather than repeatedly clicking Resume. A Mission with a completed or failed report is read-only; do not assume it can be resumed.
