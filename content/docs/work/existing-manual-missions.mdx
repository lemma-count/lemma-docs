This guide applies to an existing manual Mission. Start a new recruitment with **Start recruiting**; the historical manual-create link now enters that same role-first flow.

## Before you begin

You need operator access and an existing manual Mission. Its cockpit is distinct from a Mission agent conversation.

## Inspect and control existing work

1. Open the Mission. Check **Manual Mission**, its status, and its playbook, such as **Invite, then message** or **Message connected Leads**.
2. Compare the selected and eligible candidates with the **Audience exclusions**. Excluded people remain in the recorded cohort; they are not silently removed.
3. Read **Queue truth**. Distinguish **Ready or executing**, **Waiting**, **Satisfied without a send**, **Stopped by reply or control**, **Provider-confirmed Sent**, and **Problems**.
4. Use **Pause** for an active Mission or **Resume** for a paused one, when offered.
5. Click **Open in Outbox** to inspect the exact sequence in Sending plan and review its current history.

Launch created queued work; launch itself was not a message send. Only provider-confirmed evidence supports the Sent count.

## Replies and failures

The sequence detail can offer **Retry task**, **Queue reply**, or **Refuse reply** for eligible historical work. Use the offered controls for the exact current task. A queued manual reply uses its originating Sender. Do not assume structural sequence editing is available for manual work.

For the canonical conversation review and editable response, use [Needs you and replies](/work/replies) when the conversation is available there.

## If a control is absent

Check the Mission and sequence status. Completed work or an ineligible task may be read-only. Review history and the exclusion reason before requesting another action; a prior provider outcome must not be guessed from a queue state.
