---
title: Resolve a Sequence problem
description: Start from Open → Problems, identify the real scope, and retry only when duplicate risk is understood.
---

Problems are found under **Outbox → Open → Problems**.

## Diagnose before changing setup

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the problem Sequence

Confirm the Lead, Mission, Sender, next touch, and time. A Sender issue can affect several Sequences.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the failed or blocked touch

Open **Planned touches** and read the failure reason or timing condition. A blocked prerequisite and a failed provider attempt need different recovery.

</Step>
<Step>

### Expand History and provider evidence

Determine whether LinkedIn may already have accepted the action. An unresolved provider outcome is not safe to retry.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fix the actual scope

Reconnect or resume the Sender, wait for a retry boundary, remove a Mission or Manual sending hold, correct the Lead or message before a new revision, or stop the Sequence when the intent is no longer valid.

</Step>
<Step>

### Use the available recovery

Choose **Retry task** only when the drawer offers it and the external action is duplicate-safe. A Lemma-led Sequence without an Outbox action must be handled from its Mission or escalated to Support.

</Step>
<Step>

### Verify the result

Return to **Open → Problems**. Confirm the facet, next touch, and history changed; do not rely on a success toast alone.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Common problem scopes

### Sender

Disconnected or restricted provider state, intentional pause, exhausted capacity, or retry boundary. Open [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels).

### Timing

Future schedule, closed active window, daily capacity, spacing, or an unresolved prior LinkedIn event.

### Lead

Do not contact, missing LinkedIn identity, active outreach conflict, or the wrong relationship state for the playbook.

### Message

Missing variable, rendered invitation over 200 characters, or a changed Manual revision that still needs approval.

## When not to retry

Do not retry when:

- the history does not resolve whether LinkedIn accepted the action;
- the Lead is Do not contact;
- the message or audience intent changed;
- the same action may already have happened;
- the drawer does not offer **Retry task**.

Use [Support](/reference/support) with the Sequence, timestamp, and provider evidence when the external outcome remains unclear.
