Outbox is the central view of LinkedIn Sequences. Each row represents one Lead’s complete Sequence in one Mission and shows the next known touch.

## Use the three views

- **Open** — current Sequences.
- **Needs you** — Sequences with a decision or action Lemma can currently offer.
- **Finished** — completed, replied, opted-out, or stopped outcomes.

Problems are under **Open → Problems**. Do not use **Needs you** as the generic failure queue.

## Open a Sequence

Choose a row or **Open Sequence**. The drawer shows:

- the Mission and Lead;
- Sender and next known touch;
- planned touches and conditional timing;
- inbound replies, when present;
- **History and provider evidence**;
- only the controls allowed for the current Sequence.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Controls differ by Mission type">
The Lemma-led Mission controls Mission-level approval. Under **Open**, eligible Lemma-authored Sequences also let you edit, add, or remove unsent touches; re-anchor the eligible pre-send schedule; set Sequence-wide priority; and validate the active Sequence revision. Manual Sequences can instead expose actions such as approve a revision, retry, stop, pause the Manual Mission, or queue a suggested reply. Never assume a control exists until the current surface shows it.
</HelpCallout>
