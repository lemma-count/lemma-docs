---
title: Understand Outbox views and statuses
description: Keep the top-level views, Open facets, Finished outcomes, and touch evidence separate.
---

Outbox summarizes one Sequence per Lead and Mission. The row status is not the full state of every touch.

## Top-level views

### Open

Current Sequences that are ready, scheduled, waiting, paused, or in a problem state.

### Needs you

A subset of Sequences with an action Lemma can currently offer. A problem does not automatically belong here. Lemma-led editing and validation controls live on eligible rows under **Open** and do not require the row to appear here.

### Finished

Sequences with a terminal outcome: **Completed**, **Replied**, **Opted out**, or **Stopped**.

## Open facets

- **Ready** — current prerequisites are satisfied; no external result is implied.
- **Scheduled** — the next touch has a future time.
- **Waiting** — the Sequence is waiting on a window, capacity, event, or other condition.
- **Paused** — a Manual workspace or Mission hold is active.
- **Problems** — a task is blocked or failed and needs diagnosis.

**Running** and **Sent** are not Open Sequence facets. Look inside the Sequence’s individual touches and history for in-progress or provider-confirmed delivery evidence.

## Read the Sequence detail

- **What happens next** summarizes the current known touch.
- **Planned touches** shows each touch, conditional timing, message, and Sender.
- **History and provider evidence** distinguishes an internal state from a provider-confirmed result.

A touch can be marked complete without provider confirmation. The detail calls this out explicitly; do not report it as sent.

## Time display

Outbox formats its visible timestamps and date grouping in **Europe/Paris**. The editable **Send date** field is different: it uses the browser’s local timezone before converting the value to an instant. Neither changes the Sender’s configured schedule timezone. See [How Lemma displays time](/reference/timezones).
