A Sequence is the ordered LinkedIn work for one Lead in one Mission.

## Inspect the Sequence

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the correct view

Use **Open** for current work or **Needs you** when Lemma has an action for you. Use **Open → Problems** for failures and blockers.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the row

Confirm the Lead and Mission before acting. The drawer header links back to both.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read What happens next

Check the next channel, conditional time, Sender, and current status.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read Planned touches

Inspect the messages in order. Future timing can remain conditional on an invitation acceptance, a prior confirmed send, no reply, the Sender schedule, or another state.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check History and provider evidence

Expand the history before retrying, stopping, or concluding that a LinkedIn action happened.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Lemma-led Mission approval

Return to the Mission Cockpit for **Review approval**, **Auto approval**, and the current **Approve** or **Deny** request.

This Cockpit decision is distinct from validating the message bodies of an individual Sequence in Outbox.

## Lemma-authored Sequence actions

Open an eligible Lemma-authored Sequence under **Open**. Before a touch is sent or locked, the drawer can let you:

- edit a touch body with **Save touch**;
- remove an unsent touch with **Delete touch**;
- append a LinkedIn connection or message touch with **Add a touch**;
- use **Send date** and **Reschedule** on an unfrozen touch to re-anchor the eligible pre-send chain; every eligible touch shifts by the same amount, so the selected touch lands at the requested time and Sequence spacing is preserved;
- choose **Prioritise sending** or **Normal priority** for all eligible pre-send tasks in the Sequence;
- choose **Validate sequence** to approve and freeze the current bodies for that prospect.

Each edit creates a new immutable revision; prior work remains in history. Validation is an approval boundary, not proof of an external send.

Use **Batch validate** above the Sending plan to validate selected eligible rows. After filtering Outbox to one Mission, **Validate whole mission** validates its currently eligible Lemma-authored Sequences. A Sequence that changed after display is skipped rather than silently validating a stale revision.

These controls live in **Open**. A Lemma-led Sequence does not need to appear under **Needs you** for its editor or validation controls to be available.

## Manual Sequence actions

The current drawer can offer a subset of:

- **Save new revision** — saves an edit to the current reviewable message body;
- **Approve** or **Approve and next** — approves the current revision;
- **Retry task** — safely re-queues the identified failed task;
- **Pause Manual Mission** or **Resume Mission** — changes the Manual sending hold for that Mission;
- **Stop Sequence** — stops future cold touches for this one Lead and Mission;
- **Queue reply** — queues the read-only suggested response with the originating Sender.

Only use an action when it is visible. If the drawer does not offer it, the current Sequence is not eligible for that operation.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Do not mix the two editors">
Adding, removing, rescheduling, prioritising, and batch validation apply to eligible Lemma-authored Sequences. The current Manual contract does not promise those structural controls, and a suggested reply remains read-only.
</HelpCallout>

## After an action

Confirm that:

- the Sequence state changed;
- the next touch or hold reflects the action;
- no provider-confirmed history was erased;
- the action affected only the intended Lead, Mission, or Manual sending scope.
