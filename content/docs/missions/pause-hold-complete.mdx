---
title: Pause, hold, stop, or complete work
description: Choose the smallest control that stops the intended scope without hiding in-flight work.
---

Different controls stop different work. First decide whether the scope is one Sequence, one Manual Mission, one Lemma-led Mission’s LinkedIn activity, all Manual sending, or the entire Sender.

## Pause a Lemma-led Mission after this turn

In a Lemma-led Mission, choose **Pause after this turn** when you want Lemma to stop starting new Mission turns. The current turn may finish.

This is not the fastest way to stop provider-side LinkedIn activity. Use **Hold LinkedIn** for the Mission or **Pause** for the Sender when sending must stop.

## Hold LinkedIn for one Lemma-led Mission

Open **More mission actions → Hold LinkedIn**. Provider-side LinkedIn work for that Mission is held, while Lemma can continue research and drafting.

Choose **Resume LinkedIn** after the operating reason is resolved.

## Pause one Manual Mission

Choose **Pause** from the Manual Mission, or open one Manual Sequence in Outbox and choose **Pause Manual Mission**. All Manual sending for that Mission is held. Choose **Resume** from the Mission or **Resume Mission** in the Sequence drawer to remove the hold.

## Hold all Manual sending

At the top of Outbox, choose **Hold all manual sending** to stop all Manual sending. Lemma-led work is unaffected by this global hold.

The Outbox banner shows the active hold. Choose **Resume manual sending** from that banner to remove it.

## Stop one Manual Sequence

Open the Sequence and choose **Stop Sequence**. This stops future cold touches for that exact Manual Mission and Lead. It does not stop other Leads, Missions, or Lemma-led work.

## Pause the Sender

Open [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels) and choose **Pause** when every eligible action through that LinkedIn account should stop becoming eligible. An already in-flight provider action may still need to settle.

## Complete a Lemma-led Mission

Choose **More mission actions → Complete Mission** in a Lemma-led Mission when the Mission itself should end.

Completion:

- cancels unclaimed pending, ready, blocked, and approved tasks;
- does not pretend an already-sending provider action was canceled;
- enters **Finishing** while sending work settles;
- becomes **Completed** when no sending task remains;
- preserves the plan, evidence, outreach history, and Lead memberships as read-only history.

The terminal Mission can still show a completion report. Provider-backed metrics may display an em dash when the required evidence is unavailable, and action-needed items can remain after the Mission ends.

## Archive terminal Missions

Completed and failed Missions can be archived from the expanded Mission navigation. Archiving:

- removes the Mission from **Current**;
- preserves its Lead, Outbox, conversation, and result history;
- keeps it searchable under **Archived**.

Archive from the individual Mission row only after it is terminal. Archiving is organization, not deletion, and does not rewrite provider history.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="No control rewrites provider history">
After applying a stop, inspect Outbox and the Audit log. A provider action already accepted by LinkedIn remains part of the history.
</HelpCallout>

## Emergency order

When unintended sending may be imminent:

1. use **Hold LinkedIn** for one Mission or **Pause** for the Sender;
2. use **Hold all manual sending** when the scope is every Manual Mission;
3. inspect the affected Sequences and provider evidence;
4. open **Support** from the Lemma sidebar with the Mission, Lead, Sender, and timestamp.
