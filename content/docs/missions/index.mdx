## Use the Missions workspace

Open **Missions** for the current list and workspace summary:

- Active Missions;
- Missions that need attention;
- distinct Leads in live Missions;
- Mission-attributed replies over 30 days;
- provider-verified meetings over 30 days.

Use **Performance** for aggregate Sent, Failed, Replies, reply rate, Interested, conversion rate, remaining Sender capacity, and per-Mission results. These aggregates use different definitions from the rolling distinct-Lead outcomes on Home.

Expand Mission navigation to search by name and switch between **Current** and **Archived**. Completed or failed Missions can be archived without deleting their Cockpit, Lead, Outbox, or result history.

## The two approval paths

### Lemma-led

**Review approval** and **Auto approval** are Mission controls in Cockpit. Under Review approval, Lemma presents a specific Mission-level **Approve** or **Deny** request in the conversation.

Outbox has a second, Sequence-level review surface. For eligible Lemma-authored Sequences, use **Open** to edit the unsent plan and choose **Validate sequence**, or use **Batch validate** for a selection or filtered Mission. Validation approves and freezes the current message bodies; it is not evidence that LinkedIn sent them.

### Manual

The wizard’s **Review** step calculates the exact audience and rendered messages. **Launch Mission** commits that reviewed audience and queues approved work. Do not assume a second pre-send approval always exists in Outbox.

After Launch, the Manual Cockpit shows audience counts, queue truth, exclusions, and direct Pause or Resume controls. It does not expose the Lemma-led conversation, approval mode, or More mission actions menu.

<HelpCallout type="warning" title="Launch and provider delivery are different">
Creating or launching a Mission can create and queue work. A LinkedIn action is confirmed only by provider evidence in the Sequence history or Audit log.
</HelpCallout>
