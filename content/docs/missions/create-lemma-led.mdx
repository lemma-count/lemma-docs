Choose the Lemma-led path when you know the outcome but want help clarifying the audience, research, and plan.

<Outcome title="Outcome">
Lemma creates a clarifying Mission and opens its conversation. The goal is preserved and the conversation shows what Lemma needs next.
</Outcome>

## Write the goal

Include:

- what you offer;
- who should care;
- why this audience is relevant now;
- the conversation or outcome you want;
- geography, timing, claims, tone, and eligibility constraints;
- what Lemma must not infer or promise.

<ExampleBlock title="Starting goal">
We provide fractional finance operations for small French software companies. Reach founders who recently hired their first finance employee. Start a useful conversation about month-end pressure. Keep the tone direct and do not imply guaranteed savings.
</ExampleBlock>

## Create the Mission

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open New Mission

Open [**New Mission**](https://app.heylemma.com/missions/new). The main text area is the Lemma-led path.

</Step>
<Step>

### Describe the outbound goal

Use the large text area under **What do you want Lemma to achieve?** Enter adds a new line. Use Command+Enter or Control+Enter, or choose the send icon, to start.

</Step>
<Step>

### Continue in the Mission

Lemma opens the new Mission. Answer the focused questions and correct any interpretation that changes the audience, offer, or constraints.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep Review approval while learning

Open **More mission actions** and confirm **Review approval** is selected. When Lemma requests a consequential decision, the conversation displays **Approve** and **Deny**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Approve the exact request

Read the prompt and current Mission context before choosing **Approve**. If the request is unclear or wrong, choose **Deny** or explain the correction in the conversation.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Auto approval

**Auto approval** applies only to future LinkedIn Sequences. It does not change existing decisions. Enable it only after the audience, claims, and operating boundaries are stable.

Sender state, schedule, daily targets, Do not contact, eligibility, holds, and provider conditions still apply.

## Common issues

### The goal is too broad

Use one audience and one outcome. A larger Lead pool does not repair an unclear goal.

### Lemma needs Leads

Import a bounded Lead set and, when needed, create a List. See [Leads](/leads).

### Lemma needs a Sender

Open [Settings → Sender](https://app.heylemma.com/settings/channels). A healthy verified connection normally becomes Ready automatically. Check the configured targets and current effective limits separately: automatic capacity warm-up starts with the first provider-authorized outreach activity.

### The plan includes an unsupported claim

Correct the context in the Mission conversation and inspect the available Lead evidence. Generated text is not evidence.
