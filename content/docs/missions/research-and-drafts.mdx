---
title: Inspect Mission context and prepared copy
description: Read the Mission inspector, Mission detail, and Lead provenance without treating an internal plan as provider evidence.
---

Research and generated copy are preparation. Lemma exposes different context in the Mission inspector, Mission detail, Lead drawer, and Outbox.

## Read the Mission inspector

The right side of a Lemma-led Mission contains:

- an execution-readiness line;
- provider-backed Targeted, Sent, Replied, and Meetings counts when available;
- **Objectives** derived from the current Mission;
- persistent **Lead work** cards with each Lead’s canonical LinkedIn plan and durable work state.

The inspector is the best place to understand what the Mission is trying to do and which Lead work exists. It is not a field-level provenance viewer.

## Find Mission-level context

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Mission

Open the Mission from the sidebar or a Sequence’s **Mission** link.

</Step>
<Step>

### Open More mission actions

Choose the three-dot **More mission actions** control, then **Mission details**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the available detail

Mission detail can show Goal and ICP, the success contract, and recent compressed intelligence. An empty section means that the current Mission has not recorded that material.

The drawer does not guarantee a source URL, confidence score, freshness label, or complete research record for each insight.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Find Lead-level provenance

Open [Leads](https://app.heylemma.com/leads), then open the relevant Lead. Under **Imported and inferred information**, a field can show:

- its current value;
- state;
- source;
- confidence when available;
- freshness date when available.

These structured values are read-only in the current drawer.

## Review prepared copy

Check:

- the correct Lead, Mission, and Sender;
- the claim that links the Lead’s context to the offer;
- whether the supporting field is imported, structured, or inferred;
- missing variables or generic fallbacks;
- tone, requested action, and outcome claims;
- the exact approval request or Manual review currently in front of you.

<HelpCallout type="tip" title="Use one supportable reason">
One relevant, supportable connection is better than several generic or inferred personalization lines.
</HelpCallout>

## Correct the work

- In a Lemma-led Mission, tell Lemma what is wrong in the conversation and **Deny** an incorrect approval request.
- In a Manual Mission, go back from **Review** to **Sequence**, correct the copy or variables, then calculate the exact audience again.
- In Outbox, edit only where the selected Sequence visibly offers an edit or authoring control.

There is no generic **Regenerate research** control in Mission details or the Lead drawer. Do not document a regeneration as complete unless the current UI exposes and confirms it.
