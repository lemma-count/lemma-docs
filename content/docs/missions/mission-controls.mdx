title: Use Mission controls
description: Use the controls that belong to a Lemma-led or Manual Mission.
---

The Mission page depends on how the Mission was created.

- A **Lemma-led Mission** combines the Lemma conversation, approvals, Mission details, and lifecycle controls.
- A **Manual Mission** shows operational metrics, queue truth, audience exclusions, and direct Pause or Resume controls. It does not expose the Lemma-led conversation or approval menu.

## Read a Lemma-led Mission

Read the page in this order:

1. Mission name, public status, and **Lemma-led** mode.
2. The current conversation or approval request.
3. The approval choice and next action.
4. Mission readiness and provider-backed KPI line.
5. **Objectives** and the persistent **Lead work** cards.

Each Lead work card can show the canonical LinkedIn plan, current work state, and attached worker evidence. Open the Lead or its worker only when the current card offers that action.

## Answer Lemma

Use the Mission conversation for clarification and corrections. When Review approval requires a binary decision, the request appears in the thread with **Approve** and **Deny**.

Approve only the specific request shown. A prior approval does not authorize a materially different audience, Sender, or message.

## Choose the Lemma-led approval mode

Open **More mission actions**:

- **Review approval** keeps future consequential requests waiting for your decision.
- **Auto approval** applies to future LinkedIn Sequences only. Existing approval decisions stay unchanged.

The approval mode does not override the Sender schedule, daily targets, Do not contact, Lead eligibility, holds, or provider restrictions.

## Rename a Lemma-led Mission

Choose the pencil beside the Mission name to edit the display name or regenerate one from the goal. Renaming does not change the goal, audience, Sender, Sequence, approval history, or provider evidence.

## Open Mission details

Choose **More mission actions → Mission details**. When available, the read-only drawer shows:

- Goal and ICP;
- the success contract, including definitions, deadline, and targets;
- recent compressed intelligence.

This is a compact Mission record, not a complete research-source feed. Use the Lead drawer for field-level source, confidence, and freshness information.

## Control a Lemma-led Mission

- **Pause after this turn** — the current Lemma turn may finish, but a new Mission turn does not start.
- **Resume** — continues a paused Mission.
- **Hold LinkedIn** — stops provider-side LinkedIn activity for this Mission while Lemma can continue research and drafting.
- **Resume LinkedIn** — removes that channel hold.
- **Complete Mission** — stops new Mission work and preserves the result as history.

See [Pause, hold, stop, or complete work](/missions/pause-hold-complete) before using an emergency control.

## Read a Manual Mission

After Launch, a Manual Mission shows:

- Selected, Eligible, and Blocked Leads;
- total Tasks, provider-confirmed Sent, Waiting, and Problems;
- queue-state counts;
- the blocked-audience breakdown preserved from Launch;
- direct **Pause** or **Resume** and **Open in Outbox** controls.

## Finishing, reports, and terminal states

If a provider-authorized task is already sending when you complete the Mission, it can enter **Finishing** until that in-flight task settles. **Completed** and **Failed** Missions are read-only.

A terminal Lemma-led Mission can show a completion report with:

- People targeted;
- People messaged and Actions confirmed when provider evidence exists;
- Meetings booked;
- remaining action-needed items;
- per-Lead progress and the read-only transcript.

An em dash means that provider-backed evidence was unavailable; it does not mean zero.
